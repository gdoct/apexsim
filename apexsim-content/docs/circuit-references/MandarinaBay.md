# Marina Bay Street Circuit, Singapore (`MandarinaBay`)

Modelled on: Marina Bay, Downtown Core and Kallang, Singapore. In game: 4.92 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.927 km (F1 official; announced as 4.928 km, Wikipedia lists 4.940 km for 2023-24), 19 turns, anticlockwise, night race under ~1,600 floodlights.

## Which layout
- **Current (2023-present, still used in Oct 2026):** 19 turns. The four corners on The Float at Marina Bay (the floating platform, old Turns 16-19, which ran under the Bay Grandstand) are bypassed by a 397.9 m straight along Raffles Avenue. Old Turns 20-23 became Turns 16-19. F1 gives 4.927 km; the 2022 announcement gave 4.928 km. Race grew from 61 to 62 laps.
- **The in-game 4.92 km matches the current 19-turn layout.** If the in-game track still has the floating-platform loop (cars passing under a grandstand on the water), it is the old 5.063 km layout and is wrong for the length.
- **Why:** The Float is being rebuilt as NS Square. Completion slipped from 2025 to 2026 and then 2027. An Oct 2026 report says a 2027 layout could run around a new Bay grandstand again with faster corners, but nothing is confirmed. Model the straight.
- **Earlier configurations:**
  - 2008: 5.067 km, 23 turns, with the "Singapore Sling" chicane (left-right-left) at Turn 10.
  - 2009-12: 5.073 km, Turn 10 kerbs reworked.
  - 2013-14: 5.065 km. The Sling was replaced by a single-apex left at Turn 10.
  - 2015-17: Turns 11-13 reprofiled. T11 runs on the left side of Fullerton Road, T12 uses the other (left) side of Anderson Bridge, and the T13 hairpin is 1 m wider.
  - 2018-22: 5.063 km, T16-17 reprofiled.
- No other series uses a variant.

## Corners
Directions come from the SI track guide, the Wikipedia lap description and The Race. Roads follow Oversteer48 and Wikipedia. Turns 1 (Sheares) and 7 (Memorial) were named in a 2009 competition. "Singapore Sling", "Waterside", "Garage", "Barge", "Wharf" and "Downtown" also appear as labels on the 2026 Circuit Park Map; most are hospitality or area names, and which corners they belong to (if any) is unconfirmed.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** On the left (inside) of the start/finish straight. The pit straight runs just below the Benjamin Sheares Bridge on vacant land next to the Singapore Flyer. Pole position is on the right-hand side of the grid (RaceFans; the FIA 2024 drawing says "Pole RHS").
- **Pit entry:** Before the penultimate corner (T18), on the left, just after the Skyline Grandstand. The final-corner wall was moved to make room for it. The FIA drawing shows bollards on the left side at pit entry.
- **Pit exit:** Runs along the left of the straight and rejoins at the Turn 1 braking zone on the inside (left), in front of the Pit Exit Grandstand. The pit lane is about 790 m long, and a transit with a stop takes about 30 s (Oversteer48).
- **FIA drawing labels (2024):** Pit Lane Starts / Ends, SC1 and SC2 lines, Fast Lane, Collection Point, FIA Safety & Medical Car, F1 Garages (boxes 1-41), and FIA/FOM offices.
- **F1 Pit Building:** Permanent, 1 Republic Boulevard, 3 storeys plus a rooftop Sky Deck.
  - Level 1: garages.
  - Level 2: Paddock Club suites, Media Conference Room and Media Centre (FIA press conference room).
  - Level 3: Paddock Club suites and the Singapore Suite, whose porch overlooks Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Marina Barrage and MBFC.
  - Gross floor area 24,220 m²; 1,396 solar panels on the roof.
  - Race control and the podium are in or at the building (f1technical).
  - Overall length is not published (Level 2/3 suites are about 41.6 × 22.6 m each).
- **Facing the pits:** The Pit Grandstand (with Super Pit seats in its top rows) is on the outside, right-hand side of the straight, opposite the Paddock Club.
- **Paddock:** "F1 Paddock" in Zone 4 is a restricted area behind the pit building (2026 map). A support paddock is passed on the route from Gate 2.
- **Start and finish:** The 2026 map marks "Start" and "End" separately; their exact offset is unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
Essentially flat, at sea level on reclaimed land. The only small humps are the bridge crossings (Anderson Bridge at T12-T13 and Esplanade Bridge at T13-T14). No published elevation profile was found.

## Modelling pitfalls
- The floating-platform loop under the Bay Grandstand is gone since 2023. Use the 397.9 m Raffles Avenue straight with T15 as a flat kink and T16 as a hard right. Old maps (2008-2022, 23 turns) show the platform.
- The Turn 10 "Singapore Sling" is a single-apex left since 2013, not a chicane.
- T11-T13 use the left side of Fullerton Road and Anderson Bridge (2015 change). Pre-2015 maps show the other carriageway.
- The pit lane is on the left (inside). Pit exit joins at T1 on the inside, and pit entry is before T18 on the left.
- It is a street circuit, so almost everything is temporary: grandstands, walls, fences, superscreens and the floodlight gantries. The permanent parts are the pit building, the purpose-built road for the pit straight, T1-T3 and the final turns, and the city buildings.
- The Benjamin Sheares Bridge viaduct crosses the circuit and is a key visual landmark. Getting its line wrong skews the whole T1-T3 and T17 area.
- Grandstand turn labels on official pages use the current 19-turn numbering. Older guides (enterf1, pre-2023) use 23-turn numbering.
