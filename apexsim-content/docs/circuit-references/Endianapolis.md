# Indianapolis Motor Speedway, USA (`Endianapolis`)

Modelled on: Speedway, Indiana (4790 West 16th Street, "16th & Georgetown"), west of Indianapolis, USA. In game: 4.02 km, IndyCar. Real (current layout): oval 4.023 km (2.500 mi), 4 turns, anticlockwise.

## Which layout
- **Oval (1909 to date): 2.500 mi / 4.023 km, run anticlockwise.** Rectangle-like "four-corner" oval: two straights of 0.625 mi (front stretch and backstretch, 50 ft wide, flat), two short chutes of 0.125 mi (50 ft wide, flat), and four identical turns of 0.259 mi (60 ft wide) banked 9°12'. The 1993 change grassed over the inside apron of the turns (narrowing the racing surface) and added the warm-up lane; racingcircuits.info lists separate oval maps for 1909-92, 1993-99, 2000-07, 2008-13 and 2014 to date (infield/pit changes, not the oval line). **The in-game 4.02 km matches the 2.5 mi oval used for the Indianapolis 500.**
- **Road courses (reference):**
  - 2000-2007 F1 Grand Prix course: 2.605 mi / 4.192 km, 13 turns, clockwise, infield plus oval Turn 1 (taken in reverse as the final corner) and the front stretch.
  - 2008-2013 motorcycle course (MotoGP): 2.621 mi / 4.218 km, 16 turns, anticlockwise, bypassing oval Turn 1 with the infield "Snake Pit Complex"; 2008-13 sportscar course 2.534 mi / 4.078 km (clockwise, oval Turn 1 as the last corner).
  - **2014 to date IndyCar road course (Sonsio Grand Prix): 2.439 mi / 3.925 km, 14 turns, clockwise.** Repaved October 2013. Turn 1 is a 90-degree corner with a raised inside kerb; the two slow turns inside oval Turn 4 were bypassed; a new 90-degree left at the end of the Hulman Boulevard backstretch leads into faster turns behind the Museum; two tight corners were replaced by a single 90-degree right to the pit entry; it uses the "Snake Pit" infield complex inside oval Turn 1 instead of the oval Turn 1 banking.
  - 2014 to date motorcycle course: 2.591 mi / 4.170 km, 16 turns, runs the opposite way to the road course.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Start/finish:** on the front stretch at the Yard of Bricks (same line for oval and road course; road-course start/finish position on the bricks unconfirmed).
- **Pit lane:** on the inside (infield, east) of the front stretch, with a row of 37 pitside garages built in 2000 along the main straight; pit lane, warm-up lane and the grassed apron since 1993.
- **Pit entry (oval):** off Turn 4 on the inside, **pit exit** into Turn 1 on the inside (standard IMS practice; unconfirmed from a primary text here). Road course: pit entry after a 90-degree right.
- **Pagoda (1998-2000 renovation):** race control and the media centre; on the inside of the front stretch by the pit lane, with **Pagoda Plaza** (Gallagher Pavilion / Green Flag Club) behind it. Height/floors unconfirmed. Replaced the 1957 Master Race Control Tower, which replaced the 1926 Pagoda.
- **Gasoline Alley:** garage complex in the infield behind the pits: three buildings of 32 stalls (96 garages, 1986), plus 37 pitside garages = 133 stalls.
- **Victory Podium / Victory Lane:** on the tour route with the Pagoda and Gasoline Alley; exact position unconfirmed (by the Pagoda).
- **Scoring pylon:** not confirmed from a source here (known landmark on the inside of the front stretch; position unconfirmed).

## Terrain and elevation
- Flat site; the only relief is the 9°12' banking of the four turns; straights and short chutes are flat (en.wikipedia). Absolute elevation unconfirmed (~220 m above sea level, unconfirmed).

## Modelling pitfalls
- The oval is not an ellipse: four distinct 9°12' turns joined by two long straights and two 1/8-mile flat short chutes.
- The oval runs anticlockwise; the 2014 IndyCar road course runs clockwise (front stretch northward), and its last corners use the Snake Pit infield complex, not the Turn 1 banking (the 2000-07 F1 course did use the banking in reverse).
- The inside apron of the turns has been grass since 1993; the warm-up lane and pit lane are on the inside.
- The Yard of Bricks is a strip across the track at the start/finish line, not the whole straight.
- The Pagoda is a 2000 building; older images show the 1957 control tower.
- "Snake Pit" means two things: the motorcycle/road-course infield complex inside Turn 1 and the race-day concert area in the infield.
