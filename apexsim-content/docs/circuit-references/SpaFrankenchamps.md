# Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps (`SpaFrankenchamps`)

Modelled on: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps, in the Ardennes forest between Francorchamps, Stavelot and Malmedy (province of Liège), Belgium. In game: 6.99 km, F1. Real (current layout): 7.004 km, 19 turns (F1 numbering), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current: 2007-present, 7.004 km, 19 turns.** For 2007 the Bus Stop was replaced by a new, tighter chicane (moved back toward Blanchimont), La Source was reprofiled/moved forward, and a **new pit building, pit lane and paddock** were built on the straight between the chicane and La Source. The start/finish has been on that straight (before La Source) since 1981.
- 2022 renovation (FIA Grade 1 + FIM, ~EUR 80m): gravel traps returned at La Source, Eau Rouge/Raidillon (T2-T4 run-offs widened, rails moved), Les Combes/Malmedy (T6-T7), Bruxelles (T8), Blanchimont (T16-T17); new 4,600-seat covered grandstand with VIP boxes at the top of Raidillon; new La Source terraces/grandstands (+7,300 places) replacing the old 24H grandstand. Track line unchanged.
- 2026 (FIA competition notes): only detail changes — wall extended on the right approaching T2, rain grooves in the Kemmel straight, T5 apex grasscrete replaced by asphalt, T14 bump ground down, T19 apex kerb smoothed, a new gate on the right of the pit exit road. The old 1985 control tower was being demolished from Nov 2025 for the new "Uhoda Tower" (opening planned June 2026).
- Earlier: 2004-2006 6.976 km (Bus Stop reprofiled with an extra right sweep); 1995-2003 6.968 km; 1994 7.001 km (temporary tyre chicane at Eau Rouge); 1981-1993 6.940 km (original Bus Stop, start/finish moved before La Source); 1979-1980 6.947 km (new permanent link Les Combes-Paul Frere, no Bus Stop); 1957-1978 ~14.1 km road circuit via Burnenville, Malmedy, Masta, Stavelot.
- **Motorcycle variant (2022-present): 6.985 km, 20 turns**, with a chicane/bypass at the Jacky Ickx corner (T9 area).
- **In-game 6.99 km sits between 6.976 km (2004-06), 6.985 km (bike layout) and 7.004 km (current).** Check the Bus Stop shape: if it has the 2004-06 extra sweep or the pre-2007 hairpin-type Bus Stop, the game models a pre-2007 track (and the pit lane would be wrong too).

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Start/finish straight** runs from the Bus Stop (T19) to La Source (T1). **Pit lane and 2007 pit building on the right (inside)** of the straight; Gold 1 and Gold 9 grandstands face them from the left (outside), looking at grid, pit lane, podium and parc ferme.
- **Pit entry:** on the right before the Bus Stop chicane; passing right of the bollard at Safety Car Line 1 = entering the pits (FIA 2026 notes). The pit lane skips the chicane.
- **Pit exit:** the pit lane runs past the start line and La Source on the inside; cars leave via a **pit exit road on the right after T1**, separated from the track by a white line, and must cross it at the earliest opportunity. Practice starts on the right of the pit exit road after T1. At the end of the race cars turn right into the pit exit after T1 and use the fast lane to parc ferme. Pit loss ~19 s (pit exit after La Source).
- **Pit building:** 2007 complex with the F1 paddock behind it (inside the straight). Media Centre is "in the F1 Paddock" with the photographers' area beside it (FIA media kit); exact floor unconfirmed. Length and storeys unconfirmed. Podium location: visible from Gold 1 (on the pit building side, unconfirmed exactly).
- **Race control:** not in the old control tower (unused for years); its location is unconfirmed. The old 1985 control tower is being replaced by the Uhoda Tower (offices/events and a sim centre), probably the "Business Tower" on the 2026 wayfinding map (unconfirmed).
- **Old/Endurance pits:** the original pit buildings along the downhill between La Source and Eau Rouge (site of 1949 pits and the 1956 Englebert Tower); used as the F2/F3 support pits during the GP (visible from Gold 2) and labelled "Endurance Pit" on the access plan.
- **Support paddock:** next to the old pits; two underpasses link the Eau Rouge area to the F1 and support paddocks (spectator comment). The Rohon stream runs under the paddock.

## Terrain and elevation
About 100 m between the highest point (Les Combes, T5-T7 area) and the lowest (Stavelot/Campus, T14-T15). Main features: descent from La Source to Eau Rouge; Raidillon climb (15-18%, ~40 m) to the crest at T4; Kemmel straight continues climbing to Les Combes; long descent through Malmedy, Bruxelles, Pouhon and Fagnes down to Campus/Stavelot; then the long gradual climb via Paul Frere and Blanchimont back up to the Bus Stop and La Source.

## Modelling pitfalls
- 6.99 km in game does not match 7.004 km: verify the Bus Stop and La Source are the 2007 versions and that the pit lane runs along the straight before La Source.
- Two pit complexes: the 2007 F1 pits on the start/finish straight and the old pits on the downhill after La Source (F2/F3/endurance). Don't put the F1 garages on the downhill.
- Pit exit joins **after La Source** on the right via a long pit exit road; pit entry is before the Bus Stop, so pitting skips both the chicane and T1.
- Pre-2007 maps show the old Bus Stop (a tight double chicane closer to the end of the lap) and a different pit layout.
- Run-offs changed from tarmac to gravel in 2022 at T1, T2-T4, T6-T7, T8, T16-T17.
- Raidillon grandstand (2022) and La Source terraces are new; the old 1985 control tower is gone (2025-26).
- Many grandstands are temporary for the GP; Raidillon grandstand is permanent.
- Corner counts vary (F1: 19; Audi article: 20; bike layout: 20).
