# Motorsport Arena Oschersleben (`Oskarsleben`)

Modelled on: Motorsport Arena Oschersleben (opened 1997 as Motopark Oschersleben), Motopark Allee 20-22, 39387 Oschersleben (Bode), Börde district, Saxony-Anhalt, Germany, ~30 km west-south-west of Magdeburg. In game: 3.69 km, DTM. Real (current layout): 3.696 km (car "A-Kurs", 90-degree Turn 1), 14 corners (7 left, 7 right, per the circuit; lapmeta counts 13), clockwise (lapmeta).

## Which layout
- **Current car configuration (since 2007):** A-Kurs 3.696 km. For 2007 the start/finish straight was extended and the old rounded/S-shaped Turn 1 replaced by a sharp 90-degree left followed by a right that blends back into the old line (the Hotel corner); a tarmac run-off was added outside the Triple curves so cars rejoin at the Hotel corner; a new asphalt run-off and gravel trap were added at Turn 1. The tight 90-degree Turn 1 is notorious for first-lap pile-ups.
- **Motorcycle / old configuration:** A-Kurs with the S-curve Turn 1, 3.667-3.668 km (1997-2006 for cars; still used by motorcycles). Wikipedia notes some car races have also been run on this 3.668 km version.
- **Other variants:** B-Kurs 2.435 km per the circuit (2.600-2.603 km per racingcircuits.info; short link behind the Hasseröder-Kurve), C-Kurs 1.555 km per the circuit (1.100 km per racingcircuits.info). Separate 1,018 m kart track and off-road area (since 2003) on site.
- **Earlier changes:** 1997/98 pit exit extended so cars join in the middle of Turn 1 (previously before Turn 1). 2000 new extended pit entry skirting the run-off of the final corner.
- **Which one the game matches:** 3.69 km = the current 3.696 km A-Kurs with the 90-degree Turn 1, the DTM layout (DTM 2000-01, 2004-15, 2023-present; BMW and DTM press releases quote 3.696 km).

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** most likely the **left (outside) of the start/finish straight** in the clockwise direction: the pit exit merges into the middle of the left-hand Turn 1 and the pit entry skirts the run-off of the final corner, and the start/finish grandstand stands in the infield with the fan zone behind it (ADAC 2024). Not stated outright by any source: treat as inferred.
- **Pit entry:** extended entry around the outside run-off of the final corner (since 2000).
- **Pit exit:** joins mid-Turn 1 (since 1997/98). The ~9 m DEKRA Fan Tower overlooks the pit exit (2026).
- **Pit building:** 29 garages, offices, VIP lounges and a bistro above the garages; large paddock behind. Storeys unconfirmed (at least 2).
- **Race control:** tower adjoining the pit building, with digital video monitoring, commentary boxes and a press centre for ~100 media (racingcircuits.info).
- **Hotel:** 4-star circuit hotel adjacent to the pit facilities (88 rooms per racingcircuits.info, 95 per Bridebook, 100 per de.wikipedia), with a Sky Lounge and rooftop terrace on the 3rd floor overlooking the track. Exact position along the pits (likely the Turn 1 end, given the corner name) unconfirmed.
- **Paddock:** behind the pit building; fuel station; shower and toilet in each garage box. Parc fermé and scrutineering in the paddock.
- **Start/finish line, podium:** positions unconfirmed.
- **Medical Center:** on site (rescue centre); position unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
Built on flat Börde farmland (described as "topfeben"), but the circuit quotes a 23 m elevation difference (lapmeta: 21 m). Hasseröder-Kurve is slightly raised/banked. Many bumps (BMW). Spectator areas are raised 6-8 m above the track on earth banks, giving the arena/bowl character.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Two Turn 1 shapes: the car layout (3.696 km) has a sharp 90-degree left; the bike layout and pre-2007 maps (3.667 km) show a rounded/S Turn 1. The game's 3.69 km means the 90-degree version.
- Pre-2000 maps show the old short pit entry; pre-1998 maps show the pit exit before Turn 1 instead of mid-Turn 1.
- The track is compact with many corners and few straights; the Triple run-off (tarmac since 2007) connects back to the Hotel corner.
- Spectator banks sit 6-8 m above the track everywhere: the venue reads as a bowl even though the track itself is nearly flat.
- Corner count differs by source (13, 14, 15): the circuit's own figure is 14 (7 L, 7 R).
