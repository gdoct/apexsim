# Nürburgring Nordschleife, Germany (`Mordschleife`)

Modelled on: Nürburg / Adenau (Eifel), Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany. In game: 20.76 km, Endurance. Real (current layout): 20.832 km, 154 turns (en.wikipedia count), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Nordschleife alone ("Nordschleife with Kurzanbindung"), 1983 to date: 20.832 km** (en.wikipedia also quotes 20.830 km; nuerburgring.de lists 20.832 km). Since the GP-Strecke was built (1982-84) the Nordschleife is closed by a short link at its southern end: from the end of Döttinger Höhe through Antoniusbuche, Tiergarten and the Hohenrain-Schikane to **T13**, then the Sabine-Schmitz-Kurve and Hatzenbach. The old Start-und-Ziel-Schleife / Südkehre and the Südschleife no longer exist as a lap.
- **The in-game 20.76 km is this Nordschleife-alone loop** (0.3% short of 20.832 km, plausibly centreline vs. official measurement).
- Earlier: 1927-1966 22.800 km, 1967-1982 22.835 km (with the 1967 Hohenrain chicane before the old start/finish), both including the old start/finish loop. The track was widened and smoothed in 1970-71 (e.g. the bridge at Brünnchen over a path was removed, the Schwalbenschwanz bridge replaced by a culvert).
- **24h race / combined layout: 25.378 km** = Nordschleife + GP-Strecke without the Mercedes-Arena (cars leave the Nordschleife at Hohenrain/T13 onto the GP circuit and rejoin before Hatzenbach). NLS uses 24.433 km (GP without the Müllenbachschleife).
- **Touristenfahrten (public laps)** run from the Nordschleife access area beside Döttinger Höhe (L93 roundabout, toll barrier) around to the end of Döttinger Höhe. The "Bridge to Gantry" (BTG) lap is timed from the first bridge after the barriers (Antoniusbuche) to the last gantry on Döttinger Höhe, so it is shorter than 20.8 km. Drivers leave at Döttinger Höhe (existing exit to the car park, a direct exit to the L93 roundabout, and a new exit to the L93 for busy days, built for the 2025 season).
- Since 2021 the first corner after T13 is officially the **Sabine-Schmitz-Kurve**.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Nordschleife-alone races and the in-game lap start at T13.** T13 is named after grandstand 13 ("T" = Tribüne); it was the main public entrance for laps until the "Grüne Hölle" complex and new entrance at Döttinger Höhe opened around 2000. It has its own pit lane: the **T13 Pitstop Area** (since 2023 for Touristenfahrten), entered at the Hohenrain-Schikane and rejoining before the Sabine-Schmitz-Kurve, ~100 vehicles, two pay-at-pump petrol pumps and a toilet block, 70 then 50 km/h limit.
- **For the 24h race and NLS** the pit lane, pit building (33 garages), race control and paddock are the GP-Strecke's (see `Nuerburgerring`); cars reach them by leaving the Nordschleife at Hohenrain/T13.
- **Touristenfahrten:** entry from the L93 roundabout between Nürburg and Meuspath, through a toll barrier, onto the end of Döttinger Höhe; exits at Döttinger Höhe to the car park or the L93; mobile barrier on Döttinger Höhe for repeat laps.
- **Media tower:** the Nordschleife media tower (webcam) overlooks the start/finish straights, the paddock and part of Hatzenbach (exact position at the GP/T13 junction, unconfirmed).

## Terrain and elevation
- Nearly 300 m between the lowest point at Breidscheid (Adenau) and the highest at Hohe Acht (en.wikipedia, de.wikipedia); gradients over 16% before Hohe Acht (17% planned, 11% descents).
- Main descent: Hatzenbach/Flugplatz down through Fuchsröhre to Adenauer Forst, then Wehrseifen down to Breidscheid. Main climb: Ex-Mühle, Bergwerk, Kesselchen, Klostertal to the Karussell and Hohe Acht. Then gradual descent via Brünnchen and Pflanzgarten (jumps) to Schwalbenschwanz and Döttinger Höhe (slightly uphill then down to Antoniusbuche).

## Modelling pitfalls
- Do not model the pre-1983 22.8 km lap (Südkehre, old pits beside the straight); the Nordschleife-alone lap closes through Antoniusbuche-Tiergarten-Hohenrain-T13.
- T13 is both a grandstand name and a corner; the T13 pit lane is a separate parallel lane, not the GP pit lane.
- The 24h layout uses the GP circuit without the Mercedes-Arena; the Nordschleife-only lap does not touch the GP circuit.
- Bridges: the track passes over the B257 at Quiddelbacher Höhe and under road bridges at Breidscheid and Antoniusbuche; the Brünnchen and Schwalbenschwanz bridges were removed in 1970-71.
- Touristenfahrten maps start/end at Döttinger Höhe, not at T13; BTG lap length is shorter than the racing lap.
- The Karussell is concrete slabs, banked; the Kleines Karussell is a smaller banked 90-degree left.
- Corner numbering varies by source (oversteer48 numbers from Antoniusbuche = 1); the "154 turns" figure counts every kink.
