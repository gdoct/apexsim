# Circuit de la Sarthe, Le Mans, France (`Lemons`)

Modelled on: Le Mans, Sarthe, France (south of the city, between Le Mans, Mulsanne, Arnage and Ruaudin). In game: 13.62 km, WEC. Real (current layout): 13.626 km, 38 turns (en.wikipedia), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current 24 Hours layout (2018 to date): 13.626 km** (en/fr.wikipedia; ACO's 2015 article says 13.629 km, racingcircuits.info lists 13.629 km for 2007 onward; the 3 m drop came from the 2017 Porsche Curves rework). **The in-game 13.62 km matches the current layout.**
- About three quarters of the lap is public road closed for the race: the D338 (Ligne Droite des Hunaudières / Mulsanne Straight) from Tertre Rouge to Mulsanne, the D140 past the golf course (Mulsanne to Indianapolis), the D139 from Arnage. The permanent parts are the pit straight, Dunlop, the Esses and Tertre Rouge (shared partly with the Bugatti Circuit), the Indianapolis/Arnage section rebuilt in 2016 and the Porsche Curves to the Ford chicane (1972 private bypass).
- Key changes: 1932 link road with Tertre Rouge and the Dunlop bridges; 1968 Ford chicane; 1972 Porsche Curves bypassing the old Maison Blanche kink; 1979 Tertre Rouge re-profiled (new ring road), second Dunlop bridge removed; 1986 Mulsanne corner moved for a roundabout (new right kink before it); 1987 Dunlop chicane; **1990 two chicanes on the Mulsanne Straight** (now Daytona and Michelin chicanes; earlier "Nissan" and "Carte S"); 1994 Dunlop chicane tightened; 2002 Dunlop bridge to Esses rebuilt with fast sweepers; 2006-07 Dunlop curve and Tertre Rouge re-profiled; 2016 Arnage roundabout and the Indianapolis/Arnage section; 2017 Porsche Curves safety rework (corner before Karting); **2018 start line moved 145 m forward** on the pit straight.
- **Bugatti Circuit: 4.185 km**, permanent, shares the pit complex, pit straight, Dunlop curve, Dunlop chicane, Dunlop bridge and Ford chicane with the 24h layout; leaves at La Chapelle (right) where the 24h layout goes left to the Esses; rejoins at the Ford chicane via Garage Vert, the S du Garage Bleu and the Raccordement. Has a second pit exit past the Dunlop chicane. Also the Maison Blanche driving-school circuits and the Karting des 24 Heures.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane and pit building** are on the right side of the pit straight (inside of the clockwise lap); the main grandstands are opposite (outside/left), with a lower concrete-step general-admission level facing the garages. Pit buildings of 1991 sit further back from the track; garages extended 2006 to 56 and to **60 garages by 2016**. Pit wall replaced by plate glass in 2009 on the spectator side. Storeys: multi-storey with hospitality above (count unconfirmed).
- **Pit entry:** on the right before the Ford chicane (moved in 1990); braking for the Ford chicane starts "near the beginning of the pitlane on the right".
- **Pit exit:** rejoins on the right at the Dunlop curve (white exit line); Bugatti-only second exit past the Dunlop chicane.
- **Start vs finish:** the start line was moved 145 m forward in 2018; the finish line is ~170 m after the start of the pit straight (fr.wikipedia). Start gantry on the pit straight.
- **Race control / medical post:** in the control tower built with the new grandstands (ACO).
- **Paddock:** behind the pits (inside); **Village** (shops, restaurants, Welcome hall; rebuilt 2005-06) near the straight behind the grandstands on the outside (fr.wikipedia, ACO: "not far from the straight"). Podium position unconfirmed (after the finish spectators opposite the garages are let onto the track).
- **Museum:** Musée des 24 Heures du Mans / M24 (8,600 m², reopened 28 May 2026) at the main entrance; its building required the Bugatti Circuit's parabolic curve.

## Terrain and elevation
- Largely flat; the notable relief is the rise after the Dunlop chicane cresting under the Dunlop bridge and the descent through the Esses to Tertre Rouge (Motor Sport lap guide). No elevation figures found (unconfirmed). The Mulsanne hump was lowered in 2000-01 (racingcircuits.info).

## Modelling pitfalls
- Two Mulsanne chicanes since 1990; pre-1990 maps show a straight of ~6 km.
- The Indianapolis/Arnage section and the Arnage roundabout changed in 2016; the Porsche Curves were reworked in 2017.
- The start line (2018) is not the finish line: the finish is near the start of the pit straight, the start 145 m further.
- The Dunlop bridge is a single footbridge after the chicane on a crest; older maps show a second bridge (removed 1979) and the pre-1987 curve without the chicane.
- The pit straight has the pit lane on the right; the Bugatti second pit exit rejoins past the Dunlop chicane.
- Most of the lap is public road with roadside furniture, roundabouts (Mulsanne, Arnage) and junctions; the Mulsanne corner is a road junction with a roundabout.
- Corner counts differ by source (38 per ACO/Wikipedia, 33 per sim guides).
