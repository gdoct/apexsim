# Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, Mexico City (`HermanosRodriQueso`)

Modelled on: Magdalena Mixhuca sports city, Iztacalco borough, south-east Mexico City, 2,238 m above sea level. In game: 4.28 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.304 km, 17 turns (10 right, 7 left), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current F1 Grand Prix circuit (2015-present):** 4.304 km, 17 turns. Hermann Tilke rebuilt the track for F1's return in 2015.
  - The front straight was extended and reprofiled for a new pit building, media centre and paddock.
  - The esses between T7 and T13 were changed from high-radius sweeps to fixed-angle turns.
  - The old baseball-field (Foro Sol) section became a low-speed sequence through the middle of the stadium. It bypasses the first half of the Peraltada; cars rejoin the banked corner halfway round (T17).
  - About 170 m shorter than the 1986-2014 layout.
- Turn names: T12 was named after Adrián Fernández in 2016, and T17 after Nigel Mansell in 2015.
- The Foro Sol stadium is now branded **Estadio GNP Seguros**.
- 2025 changes were minor: asphalt grinding at T12 and blue lines at T3, T5, T6 and T9.
- **Earlier configurations:**
  - 1959-85: 5.000 km, 14 turns, with the full Peraltada as the last corner.
  - 1986-2014: 4.421 km, 14 turns. F1 ran here 1986-92 with a new pit complex. The Foro Sol was built inside the Peraltada after 1992.
  - CART 2002-05 cut through the Foro Sol.
  - NASCAR 2005-14: about 4.05 km, with a main-straight chicane.
- **Other variants:** National circuit 3.909 km / 15 turns (3.850 km without the Foro Sol), the 1.609 km oval, and Formula E 2.606 km (2026). Formula E runs the oval plus a loop inside the stadium, but not between the two stadium halves.
- **The in-game 4.28 km is 24 m (0.6 %) short of the real 4.304 km.** It should be the 2015+ layout through the stadium. If the full Peraltada is used, the model is the pre-2015 layout.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** Right (inside) of the main straight. Grandstand 1 is on the left-hand side of the straight, from the T17 exit to the start line, directly opposite the pit lane and garages. Grandstand 2A is on the left about two-thirds of the way down. The FIA 2024 drawing marks "Pole LHS".
- **Pit entry:** From the Peraltada (T17) area on the right/inside. FIA 2025 notes say that passing to the right of the bollard at pit entry counts as entering the pit lane, and that tyres must not cross the white line on the left-hand side of the pit entry road. Exact branch point is unconfirmed (before or within T17).
- **Pit exit:** Runs along the right of the main straight, separated by a white line, and merges onto the straight before the T1 braking zone (unconfirmed exact point). Practice starts use painted boxes in the merging lane to the right of the fast lane.
- **Pit building:** Built in 2015 (Tilke). Garages on the ground floor; the FIA 2024 drawing shows garage numbers 01-33 (Mercedes, Ferrari, Red Bull, Sauber, Racing Bulls, Williams, Aston Martin, McLaren, Alpine, Haas), FOM, SC1/SC2 lines and the FIA Safety & Medical Car collection point. Storeys and length are unconfirmed. The 2015 works also added a hospital, hospitality and VIP area, and a media centre.
- **Paddock:** Behind the pit building, inside the straight. Gate 6 is Paddock Club VIP only.
- **Podium:** In the Foro Sol stadium near T13, between the Paddock Club buildings, not at the pit building. This is unusual and important for modelling.

## Terrain and elevation
Flat. The whole site is on the former lake bed of the Valley of Mexico at about 2,238 m (7,343 ft), the highest altitude on the F1 calendar, which matters for engine and aero. No significant gradients. The Peraltada's banking is slight (reduced to about 3° by 1992).

## Modelling pitfalls
- The current lap does not use the full Peraltada. It cuts right into the stadium at T12, winds through the middle of the Foro Sol / Estadio GNP Seguros (T13-T15), and rejoins the Peraltada halfway (T16-T17). Pre-2015 maps show the full banked corner.
- The podium and Paddock Club buildings are in the stadium near T13, not on the pit straight.
- The pits are on the right (inside) and the main grandstands on the left. Pole is LHS.
- The 1.29 km straight is very long. The 2015 Tilke esses (T7-T11) are fixed-angle turns, not the old sweeping esses.
- The stadium section is tight and walled, with grandstands rising on both sides. The model needs the stadium bowl around the track.
- Altitude of 2,238 m: the server's air model should use thin air (about 22 % lower density) if conditions are baked per track.
