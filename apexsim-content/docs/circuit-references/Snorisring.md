# Norisring (`Snorisring`)

Modelled on: Norisring, Nuremberg (Nürnberg), Bavaria, Germany - temporary street circuit on the former Nazi party rally grounds (Reichsparteitagsgelände) between the Zeppelinfeld and the Dutzendteich lake. In game: 2.28 km, DTM. Real (current layout): 2.300 km, 8 turns (Wikipedia count; Audi describes "four turns": 2 hairpins, 1 S, 1 sweeper), anticlockwise (inferred: both hairpins and the final kink are left-handers; no source states the direction outright).

## Which layout
- **Current configuration (since 1972):** 2.300 km. After Pedro Rodríguez's fatal crash in 1971 (into a bridge wall on the back section, before what is now the Schöller-S) the Grundig hairpin on Beuthener Straße was moved closer to cut speeds, and the old long straights were removed. Unchanged since 1972 apart from barriers.
- **Earlier configurations:** 1947 2.000 km (motorcycles, around the Steintribüne); 1948 and 1961-71 (sportscars) 3.940 km, 8 turns; 1949-51 4.000 km, 10 turns, with an underpass (1949) and experimental figure-of-eight forms; 1960 and the 1966 touring-car event 1.620 km short street circuit. Racing paused 1958-59.
- **Which one the game matches:** 2.28 km = the current 2.300 km layout (the only one raced by DTM, annually since 2000; the Porsche Carrera Cup page quotes "just under 2.2 km", treat as approximate).
- **Variants:** none in current use. The track is built on public roads, mainly **Zeppelinstraße** and **Beuthener Straße** (Porsche); construction starts about two to three weeks before the event and is removed within a week after (around 10,000 working hours, 600 helpers; ~7 km of crash barrier, ~6 km of catch fencing).

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Start/finish:** on the straight in front of the Steintribüne; the 2026 Lageplan places "FINISH START" next to the ADAC pedestrian tunnel and the Milwaukee-Tribüne. Start and finish are shown as one line; Pre-start 1 and Pre-start 2 areas are by the DTM pits.
- **Pit lane:** temporary, along the start/finish straight opposite the Steintribüne, on the Zeppelinfeld side (inferred: the Boxengassen-Tribüne overlooks it; "Leistner Lounge" sits above the pit area; the Zeppelinfeld "behind the track" holds TV production and press). If the lap is anticlockwise with the tribune in the infield, the pit lane is on the **right (outside)**; unconfirmed. Pit entry ("Pit approach" on the Lageplan) and pit exit positions: unconfirmed.
- **Pit building:** none permanent; temporary garages ("DTM-Pits") with a lounge above. Storeys unconfirmed.
- **Race control / race office, media centre, media accreditation:** listed on the 2026 Lageplan legend; exact positions unconfirmed from text.
- **Paddock:** several paddocks on the Lageplan (DTM, DTM Classic, ADAC GT4 Germany, BMW M2 Cup, Porsche Carrera Cup Deutschland), team hospitalities, motorhome parking, parc fermé, MCN/ADAC paddock manager, fuel station. Access for organisation/participants only via Hans-Kalb-Straße. Paddock on the Zeppelinfeld side (inferred from the history and the pedestrian tunnel); unconfirmed per zone.
- **Podium:** unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
Essentially flat (city park/rally-ground site); no elevation figure found. The surface is bumpy and undulating (BMW, endurance-info), with heavy, bumpy braking zones into both hairpins; wide track with one clean line.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Everything except the roads and the Steintribüne/Zeppelinfeld stone structures is **temporary**: grandstands, pit garages, paddock, fan zone, DEKRA tower, barriers and fencing exist only for the race weekend.
- The Steintribüne is not the intact 1930s structure: no colonnades (gone 1967), no side towers (gone 1976); it is under restoration with its middle section closed (2026).
- Pre-1972 maps show the longer layout with the Grundig hairpin further out on Beuthener Straße.
- Both hairpins are left-handers; the lap is short (~47-48 s) with only two real braking zones plus the Schöller-S.
- Walls and guardrails right at the track edge; no run-off; public-road camber and bumps.
- Orientation (which straight runs which side of the tribune, where pit entry/exit are) is not confirmed from text: check against the 2026 Lageplan PDF and satellite imagery before modelling.
