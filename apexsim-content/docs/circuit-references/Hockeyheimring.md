# Hockenheimring Baden-Württemberg (`Hockeyheimring`)

Modelled on: the Hockenheimring, Am Motodrom, 68766 Hockenheim, Baden-Württemberg, Germany, between Mannheim and Karlsruhe beside the A6. In game: 4.56 km, F1 (2002 GP layout, Motodrom stadium, Mercedes grandstand). Real (current layout): 4.574 km, 17 turns (11 right, 6 left), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Grand-Prix-Kurs, 2002 to present: 4.574 km, 17 turns (6 left, 11 right), clockwise** (official Daten & Fakten).
  - Rebuilt from February 2002 to Hermann Tilke's design, finished for the German GP of 28 July 2002.
  - **Kept**: the Motodrom stadium (1965-66, John Hugenholtz) and a tighter T1 (Nordkurve).
  - **Cut**: most of the high-speed forest loop: the long straights, the Jim Clark and Senna chicanes and the Ostkurve chicane. The forest was replanted.
  - **Added**: a short link right after T1, the new **Parabolika** straight with a small kink, the **Spitzkehre hairpin (T6)**, and a right-left-right complex (T7-T11) on the return to the stadium.
  - Capacity rose from 83,000 to about 120,000. The Mercedes-Tribüne and the enlarged Südtribüne were built for 2002.
- **Since 2002** the corner sequence is unchanged; only kerbs, run-offs and barriers have changed.
  - T1: double negative kerbs and a concrete run-off.
  - T6 and T11: domed exit kerbs.
  - T12: gravel trap on the exit.
  - T13: negative kerb on the entry, right-hand side.
  - The barrier between T11 and T12 was realigned (2019).
  - The F1 German GP was last held in 2019, and the circuit has had no F1 race since then. DTM and national series race there today.
- **Earlier layouts**:
  - 1932 "Dreieckskurs", about 12 km.
  - 1938 Kurpfalzring, about 7.5 km, with the Ostkurve.
  - 1966-2001 long circuit, about 6.8 km. The Motodrom was built after the A6 cut through the old loop; chicanes were added in 1970, 1982 and 1992.
- **Other variants today**: the National circuit (3.736 km, 15 turns), Short circuit 1 (2.638 km) and Short circuit 2 (2.604 km). The short circuits stay in the stadium area and do not use the Parabolika. There is also a rallycross layout on T11-T16 plus a dirt section in front of the stands.
- **In-game 4.56 km matches the 2002+ GP layout (4.574 km).**

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Start straight**: runs through the Motodrom from the Südkurve northwards to the Nordkurve, along the A6 side of the venue (orientation inferred from the stand names; unconfirmed).
  - The **pit lane and pit building are on the right-hand (inside) side**, between the track and the paddocks. The pit building sits "between the pitlane and paddock 2, next to paddock 1, the Sachshaus, the congress pavilion and the Baden-Württemberg-Center" (official). Those buildings are all in the infield by the Sachs-Kurve.
  - The **Haupttribüne** (LBBW-Haupttribüne) is on the **left (outside)**, at the start/finish line, facing the pits and the podium.
- **Start and finish line**: the same line; the FIA 2019 preview gives a start/finish offset of 0.000 km.
- **Pit entry**: in the Südkurve complex, on the inside (right), before the start straight. Exact point unconfirmed.
- **Pit exit**: towards the Nordkurve (T1) on the right. Exact merge point and any exit-line crossing unconfirmed; check the official plan or FIA event notes.
- **Pit lane speed**: 80 km/h (2019). Length unconfirmed.
- **Pit building ("Boxenanlage")**: one storey of garages under a large roof deck.
  - 14 triple units (210 m² each), one double (140 m²) and one single (70 m²) on the ground floor.
  - The **roof ("Boxendach") is a roughly 4,000 m² covered terrace**, an aluminium marquee about 200 m x 20 m reached by five staircases. It serves as the Paddock Club / hospitality deck.
  - "Box 47" is a hospitality unit named on the official plan.
- **Paddocks**: Fahrerlager 1, 2 and 3 (official plan). Paddock 2 is behind the pit building, by the Sachs-Kurve.
- **Race control and media centre**: positions not stated in the sources opened (unconfirmed).
- **Podium**: facing the Haupttribüne, on the pit building (unconfirmed).

## Terrain and elevation
- **Essentially flat**: about **4.3 m** of elevation change in total (F1).
- There is a slight incline up to T6 (Spitzkehre).
- The Sachs-Kurve in the stadium is **banked**.

## Key coordinates
- Circuit: **49.32778 N, 8.56583 E** (49°19′40″N 8°33′57″E, Wikipedia).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: unconfirmed. The start/finish line is in the Motodrom straight in front of the Haupttribüne.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **Do not use pre-2002 maps.** They show the 6.8 km forest loop with the Clark, Senna and Ostkurve chicanes. The 2002 layout turns right shortly after T1, runs the Parabolika and the Spitzkehre hairpin, and returns through T7-T11.
- **Mercedes-Tribüne naming**: 2019 F1 guides use "Mercedes-Tribüne" (outside T8-T9). The official 2024 plan lists an "Osttribüne" that overlooks the Parabolika and Spitzkehre, which is probably the same stand renamed (unconfirmed).
- **Corner numbering differs between sources**: the Südkurve is T17 in ticket guides and the final "T16" in some lap guides. Sponsor corner names change (Agip became Ravenol; Mobil 1, Elf, Opel).
- **The Sachs-Kurve is banked**, and the Motodrom is enclosed by stands on all sides.
- **The track is nearly flat** (4.3 m). Do not invent hills.
- **The Porsche Experience Center (2019) is in the infield.** Imagery from before 2018 shows bare ground or test areas there.
- **Temporary stands** at the Spitzkehre and Parabolika exist only for big events.
