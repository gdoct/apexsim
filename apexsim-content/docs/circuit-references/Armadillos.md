# Circuit of the Americas, Austin, USA (`Armadillos`)

Modelled on: Circuit of the Americas (COTA), south-east Austin, Travis County, Texas. In game: 5.49 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.513 km, 20 turns, anticlockwise.

## Which layout
- **Grand Prix Circuit:** 5.513 km, 20 turns, unchanged since it opened on 21 October 2012. F1, MotoGP, WEC and IMSA all use it. Designed by Hermann Tilke with HKS.
- **Other configurations:**
  - National Circuit: 3.702 km, 19 turns.
  - NASCAR Circuit (from 2025): 3.862 km, 17 turns.
  - Karting track: 1.01 km.
- No layout change is documented for 2025 or 2026. Resurfacing and bump-grinding has been done in several years, but no 2026 work could be confirmed (the web-search budget ran out before checking).
- **The in-game 5.49 km is about 23 m (0.4 %) short of the real 5.513 km.** The topology should be the full GP circuit.

## Corners
Directions come from NASCAR's turn-by-turn (full road course) and the SI track guide, which agree. The circuit's character is borrowed from other tracks: T1 from Interlagos-style turns, T3-T6 from Maggotts/Becketts, T12-T15 from the Hockenheim stadium, and T16-T18 from Istanbul Park T8.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** Left (inside) of the main straight. The Main Grandstand is on the outside (right), faces north-east, and is directly across from the pit garages and the podium. The FIA drawings mark "Pole RHS".
- **Pit entry:** After T19, on the run to T20. From the T19 apex cars head "toward the pit lane entry". Its side is not stated in text; most likely the left (inside of T20), unconfirmed.
- **Pit exit:** Runs alongside the main straight and up the hill toward T1. The Turn 1 Grandstand sees the whole of pit road, and a digital leaderboard stands at pit exit. The exact rejoin point (before or after T1/T2) is unconfirmed.
- **Pit lane:** Time loss about 20.6 s including a 2.5 s stop (F1 programme). The FIA 2024 drawing shows F1 garages 1-34, SC1/SC2 lines, the FIA Safety & Medical Car collection point, FOM and the fast lane.
- **Pit building:** A large permanent garage building with team hospitality above (two storeys plus a roof level; storey count unconfirmed). Four giant TVs are mounted along it facing the Main Grandstand, and it blocks the Main Grandstand's view of the rest of the circuit. The paddock is behind it (inside of the main straight). The media centre location is unconfirmed (likely in the paddock / pit complex).
- **Main Grandstand:** About 20 m tall, about 9,000 seats over three levels, with a tensile fabric canopy. Event spaces and lounges are on levels 2-3, plus 29 suites on level 3 and the Velocity Lounge (7,100 sq ft, 36-screen wall). Designed by Miró Rivera Architects. It runs from just after T20 to just past the start/finish line. Trackside bleachers flank it: 3 blocks west, 6 east (toward T1).
- **Start and finish:** The start/finish line is at the T1 end of the Main Grandstand, before the T1 braking zone and climb.

## Terrain and elevation
- Total elevation change about 41 m (133 ft).
- The main feature is the steep climb from the start line to T1 (gradient over 11 %), with the T1 apex on the crest as the highest point. The track then drops through T2 into the esses.
- T9 is on another crest. The back straight runs downhill to T12 (unconfirmed direction of slope), and T16-T18 drop slightly.
- No published profile was fetched.

## Modelling pitfalls
- The T1 hill is the signature. A model without a steep (over 11 %) climb and a blind crest apex is wrong.
- T13 and T14 are both rights and T15 is a tight left. Some fan guides call T12-T15 "left-right-left"; NASCAR and SI agree on L, R, R, L.
- The tower stands on the inside of the right-hand carousel (outside the circuit) and overhangs the T17 apex. The Amphitheater is at its base, and the Grand Plaza fills the arc between T15 and T19.
- The pit building is on the inside (left) of the straight and the Main Grandstand on the outside (right). Pole is RHS.
- COTALAND (between T19 and T20) is new for 2026 and absent from older imagery.
- MotoGP and the National/NASCAR layouts add shortcut links. Keep only the GP route for F1, but the short-circuit junction roads exist physically.
