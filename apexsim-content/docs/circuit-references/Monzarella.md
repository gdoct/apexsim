# Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (`Monzarella`)

Modelled on: Autodromo Nazionale Monza, inside the walled Parco di Monza (park of the Villa Reale, ~688 ha), north-east of Monza, Lombardy, Italy (~20 km north-east of Milan). In game: 5.78 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.793 km, 11 turns, clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current: Grand Prix circuit, 5.793 km, 11 turns** (since 2000; length 5.793 km quoted since 2007 when the Roggia run-off became asphalt). In 2000 the first chicane (Variante del Rettifilo) changed from a double left-right to a single right-left, and the Roggia was re-profiled.
- **2024:** full resurface; flatter, more "generic" kerbs at the Rettifilo, Roggia and Ascari chicanes (the old Ascari drain-kerbs gone); narrow gravel strips in the run-offs of the first and second chicanes; new underpass plus three enlarged ones; permanent roof over the pit building (Paddock Club previously under a tent); temporary seating replaced by more solid stands. Layout unchanged.
- **2026:** kerbs at T1, T4 and T8 rebuilt; a gravel area at T5 replaced by asphalt run-off (Pirelli/F1 preview). The outside of the Parabolica/Curva Alboreto is asphalt (gravel removed earlier). FIA 2025 notes: new gate in the pit wall in front of garage 32. **After the 2026 season** a second modernisation phase starts (no racing until July 2027): new Media Centre in the historic, unused Direzione Autodromo building near the main paddock (400 desks), permanent cover for the Paddock Club terrace on the paddock building, restyled pit building ("palazzina box"), new/updated race-control building, new grandstands, restoration of the historic Serraglio building. The works are stated not to change the track layout or main safety areas.
- Earlier: 1995-1999 5.770 km (1994 safety changes narrowed the Curva Grande, Lesmos and Parabolica for gravel); 1976-1993 5.800 km (Curva Grande chicane = Roggia, Lesmo chicane added; Rettifilo and Ascari chicanes from 1972); 1955-1971 5.750 km road course + 4.250 km banked oval (combined 10 km).
- **In-game 5.78 km falls between 5.770 km (1995-99) and 5.793 km (2000-present).** Check the first chicane: a single right-left = current; a double left-right/hairpin-like Rettifilo = pre-2000.
- **Other configurations:** Junior circuit (2.405 km, 4 turns, since 1959); the banked oval / "Sopraelevata" (4.250 km, ~30 deg banking, unused for racing since 1969-71, restored in the 2010s, used for the Monza Rally); the old circuit is labelled "Old Circuit" on the 2026 map.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit straight** runs north from the Parabolica (south end) to the Rettifilo. **Pit lane and pit building on the right (inside, east side)**; the Centrale grandstand (1), Laterale stands (4, 26A-C), Piscina (5), Vedano (24) face it from the left.
- **Pit entry:** on the right just after the exit of the Parabolica (T11) — pass right of the bollard at SC line 1. Grandstands 23A/B sit on the inside near it.
- **Pit exit:** on the right of the straight before T1; after SC line 2 a continuous white line separates the pit exit road from the track and exiting cars must stay right of it; the dotted line across the exit road marks the track edge. Grandstand 5 (Piscina) is "near the first corner and pitlane exit". Exact merge point relative to the T1 braking zone: unconfirmed.
- **Pit building:** permanent, ~46 garages historically ("palazzina box"); permanent roof added 2024 with the Paddock Club on top; due for restyling 2026-27. The podium overhangs the pit straight from the pit-building side (famous track invasion under it; side from grandstand descriptions, exact position unconfirmed).
- **Paddock:** behind (east of) the pit building ("F1 Paddock" and "Support Paddock" on the 2026 map). Media Centre ("3 Media Center" on the 2026 map) inside the paddock area for 2026; new Media Centre in the Direzione Autodromo building from 2027. Race control: in a dedicated building being rebuilt (location unconfirmed). Towers "Torre Nord" (I) and "Torre Sud" (L) labelled on the 2026 map (positions not readable from text).
- Start line vs finish: the finish is the control line on the track (FIA); separate start line not stated.
- Pit stop loss ~24 s (2026 F1 preview).

## Terrain and elevation
Essentially flat; a gentle downhill gradient from the second Lesmo to the Variante Ascari (Wikipedia). Elevation figures not confirmed.

## Key coordinates
- Wikipedia infobox: 45.62056 N, 9.28944 E (45°37'14"N 9°17'22"E).
- Start line, pit entry, pit exit: not confirmed (OSM not queryable from here). Pit entry is just after the Parabolica exit; pit exit just before T1.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Pre-2000 maps show a different first chicane (double left-right). In-game 5.78 km suggests checking this.
- The old banking is a real structure crossing over the circuit (near the Roggia braking and on the approach to Ascari) — not a separate decoration; it's closed, overgrown in parts.
- Parabolica has asphalt run-off outside (gravel removed); 2024 kerbs are flat; T5 run-off is asphalt from 2026.
- Pit lane on the right of the straight; the podium overhangs the straight.
- From late 2026 to July 2027 the paddock/media/race-control buildings are being rebuilt; 2027+ will differ.
- Many grandstands (21A-E, some Ascari stands) are temporary; 1, 22 and a few others permanent.
- One spectator guide calls the pit-straight grandstands "inside"; the FIA notes put the pit lane on the right (east), so those stands are on the west, opposite the pits.
