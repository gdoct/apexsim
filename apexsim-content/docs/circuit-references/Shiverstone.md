# Silverstone Circuit (`Shiverstone`)

Modelled on: Silverstone Circuit, the former RAF Silverstone airfield on the Northamptonshire / Buckinghamshire border near Silverstone village and Whittlebury, England, UK (A43 between Towcester and Brackley). In game: 5.88 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.891 km, 18 turns, clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current: Arena Grand Prix circuit with the Wing pits, 2011-present, 5.891 km, 18 turns.** In 2011 the start/finish line and F1 pit lane moved from the old straight between Woodcote and Copse to the new **Wing** complex on the straight between Club and Abbey (now "Hamilton Straight"), so the lap now starts at Abbey (T1). The track line is essentially the 2010 Arena layout (5.901 km measured from the old start line).
- 2010: Arena layout introduced (new infield section Abbey - Farm - Village - The Loop - Aintree - Wellington Straight - Brooklands, replacing the old Bridge/Priory section), 5.901 km, start still between Woodcote and Copse.
- 1997-2009 "Bridge GP" 5.141 km (17 turns); 1996 5.072 km; 1994-1995 5.057 km (Abbey chicane after Imola, Stowe entry modified 1995); 1991-1993 5.226 km (1990-91 redesign: Maggotts/Becketts/Chapel esses, Luffield complex replacing Bridge chicane, Club and Stowe slowed); 1987-1990 4.778 km (Bridge chicane); 1975-1986 4.719 km (Woodcote chicane); 1952-1974 4.711 km (perimeter track, start moved to Woodcote-Copse straight).
- 2025 FIA notes: kerb changes only (flat kerbs replacing red kerbs at T1, T6, T7, T9 apexes; T1 run-off kerb removed; T18-exit gravel changed to a lighter material). No layout change found for 2026.
- **In-game 5.88 km is closest to the current 5.891 km** (2010's 5.901 km and the 5.900 km motorcycle layout are further off). Check that the start/finish and pit lane are on Hamilton Straight (Wing), not between Woodcote and Copse (pre-2011).
- **Other configurations:** Motorcycle GP circuit (5.900 km, 18 turns, 2010-present; differs around Abbey/Farm, unconfirmed exactly); International circuit (2.979 km, 10 turns, 2010-present); National circuit (2.639 km, 6 turns, 1997-present, uses Copse - Maggotts - Becketts - Brooklands - Luffield - Woodcote with the old pits); Stowe circuit (small separate infield circuit, not detailed here).

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Hamilton Straight** (ex-International Pits Straight) runs from Club (T18) to Abbey (T1). **The Wing pit lane is on the right (inside)** (pit entry: pass right of the bollard at SC line 1, FIA 2025 notes). Hamilton Straight A/B grandstands are opposite.
- **Pit entry:** on the right around Club, before Hamilton Straight (exact branch point unconfirmed).
- **Pit exit:** pit exit road separated from the circuit by a white line; cars cross at the earliest opportunity; exit area behind garage 41. Rejoins on the right toward Abbey (exact point unconfirmed).
- **The Wing (2011):** 390 m long, 41 garages, a 2,780 m2 Media Centre and a new race control tower (NBS case study). Storeys and podium position unconfirmed (podium is commonly on the Wing over the pit lane; not confirmed here). F1 paddock behind the Wing (inside of the circuit); F1 Paddock Club labelled on the 2026 map.
- Finish line is the control line on the track (FIA); a separate start line is not stated in the notes.
- **Old (National) pits:** between Woodcote and Copse on the National Pits Straight (built 1952); F1 start/finish and pits until 2010; support series / national racing use (unconfirmed for the GP weekend).

## Key coordinates
- Wikipedia infobox: 52.0711 N, 1.0161 W (52°04'16"N 1°00'58"W).
- Start line, pit entry, pit exit: not confirmed (OSM not queryable from here). Start/finish on Hamilton Straight between Club and Abbey.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Pre-2011 maps put start/finish and pits between Woodcote and Copse; since 2011 they are on Hamilton Straight at the Wing.
- Pre-2010 maps show the Bridge/Priory section instead of the Arena infield (Farm - Village - Loop - Aintree - Wellington Straight).
- Two pit straights exist: Hamilton Straight (Wing, F1) and National Pits Straight (old pits).
- Corner numbering starts at Abbey, not at Copse.
- Many grandstands and all campsites/hospitality villages are temporary for the GP.
