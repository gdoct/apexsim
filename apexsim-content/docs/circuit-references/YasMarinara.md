# Yas Marina Circuit, Abu Dhabi (`YasMarinara`)

Modelled on: Yas Island, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates (purpose-built, Tilke, opened 2009; runs round a working marina and under the W Abu Dhabi - Yas Island hotel). In game: 5.52 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.281 km, 16 turns, anticlockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current Grand Prix Circuit (since the December 2021 race): 5.281 km, 16 turns.** Works designed by Mrk1 Consultants in 2021:
  - **North hairpin**: the slow old T5-T6 chicane before it was removed; the hairpin (old T7, "Stadium/North hairpin") moved slightly away from the grandstand for more run-off and its entry widened. It is now **T5**.
  - **Marsa Corner, new T9**: the four 90-degree corners old T11-T14 at the end of the second back straight replaced by one long, fast (~240 km/h) left-hand sweeping hairpin with about 5 degrees of banking.
  - **Hotel section** (old T17-T21 -> new T12-T16): kept its shape; the inside of old T17 (now T12) opened 1.5-2 m (service road kept), the two lefts opened ~2 m, old T19/T20 right opened to be full throttle. T13/T14 stay negative camber. Changes limited by the hotel, the marina retaining wall and underground services.
  - Mapping: old T1-T4 = new T1-T4; old T5/T6 gone; old T7 = T5; old T8/T9 chicane = T6/T7; old T10 = T8; old T11-T14 -> T9; old T15-T21 -> T10-T16.
- **2009-2021 Grand Prix Circuit: 5.554 km, 21 turns.**
- **The in-game 5.52 km matches the OLD 2009-2020 layout (5.554 km) far better than the current 5.281 km.** Expect the game to have the T5-T6 chicane before the hairpin and the four-corner T11-T14 complex instead of Marsa Corner. If the target is the current layout, the track is ~240 m too long and both the north and south ends must be rebuilt.
- Other layouts (2021-): Corkscrew 4.572 km/18 turns, North Circuit 3.005 km/8 turns; South Circuit (2009-2021, 2.360 km) no longer listed. A "GP Circuit with South Pits" variant uses the support pit lane under the Marina grandstand.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane on the driver's right of the main straight**; Main grandstand on the left. Pole position on the right (f1-fansite). Pit lane ~1.07 km, 80 km/h; pit loss ~20 s; 40 F1 garages (units 39-40 F1 VIP). FIA drawing labels "Pit Lane Starts/Ends", fast lane, SC1/SC2 lines, speed trap.
- **Pit entry**: on the right on the run to the last corners (exact branch point unconfirmed); visible from the West grandstand across the track.
- **Pit exit**: the famous **tunnel**: the exit lane ducks under the track at Turn 1 and comes up on the other side to rejoin on the outside around T2-T3. The pit speed limit ends before the tunnel. Built this way because there was no space outside T1.
- **Start line and finish (control) line are separate**: the finish line is at the start (T16 end) of the Main grandstand; the start line is a little further along towards T1 (Main grandstand section 1 faces the finish line, section 3 the start line). The FIA map labels "Start Line" and "Control Line" separately.
- **Pit building**: opposite the Main grandstand, three TV screens built into it; a team building sits behind it. **Podium** opposite Main grandstand section 2 (podium gantry near the finish line).
- **Paddock**: behind the pit building (infield side). **Race Control and the Media Centre face each other in the paddock** (Media Centre: work rooms first floor, press conference room ground floor). Paddock Club and Paddock Club parking listed (exact position unconfirmed).
- **Support pit lane**: the ground floor of the Marina grandstand holds the support-race garages (F2/F3/F4), along the T8-T9 straight ("South Pits").

## Terrain and elevation
Small elevation change, about 10.7-11 m. Rise through T2 over the brow of T3, drop through T4 into the hairpin; the final corner T16 drops downhill onto the straight. T9 has ~5 deg banking; T13-T14 negative camber.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **In-game length 5.52 km = old 21-turn layout.** The current layout has no chicane before the T5 hairpin and replaces old T11-T14 with banked Marsa Corner (T9). Old maps/games show 21 corners.
- Separate start line and finish line.
- Pit exit is a tunnel under T1, not a lane alongside the track.
- Two pit lanes: F1 on the main straight; support garages under the Marina grandstand.
- The W hotel spans the track (two buildings joined by a bridge over the circuit) with a lit gridshell roof; the marina sits inside the final loop.
- A bridge crosses the T8-T9 straight in front of the Marina grandstand; run-off at T6 passes under the West grandstand.
