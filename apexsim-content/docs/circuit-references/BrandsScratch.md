# Brands Hatch (`BrandsScratch`)

Modelled on: Brands Hatch, West Kingsdown (Fawkham), Kent, UK. In game: 3.90 km, DTM. Real (current layout): Grand Prix circuit 3.916 km (2.433 mi; often quoted 3.908 km), 9 turns, clockwise. Indy circuit 1.944 km (1.208 mi), 6 numbered turns.

## Which layout
- **Current configuration (since 2003):** Grand Prix circuit 3.916 km, 9 numbered turns, and the Indy circuit 1.944 km. The 2003 change reprofiled the woodland section: the old Dingle Dell chicane was removed and the complex became Sheene Curve (named after Barry Sheene, d. 2003); Dingle Dell is now a dip on a straight between Westfield and Sheene, not a corner. GP length went from 4.221 km (1999-2002, 11 turns) to 3.916 km.
- **Earlier configurations:** 1950-53 original 1.609 km kidney, anticlockwise. 1954 Druids added and direction reversed to clockwise (Paddock Hill Bend created). 1960 GP loop added (4.265 km). 1976 Paddock Bend realigned and Bottom Straight straightened to enlarge the pits; renamings: Pilgrims -> Hailwood Hill, Bottom Bend -> Graham Hill Bend, Bottom Straight -> Cooper Straight (GP 4.207 km). 1988-98 4.182 km / 11 turns, 1999-2002 4.221 km. Club circuit renamed Indy circuit in 1978.
- **Which one the game matches:** 3.90 km = the current GP circuit (3.908-3.916 km). DTM raced the **Indy** circuit 2006-2013 and the **GP** circuit in 2018 and 2019 (2020 GP races cancelled). The game's 3.90 km therefore matches the 2018-19 DTM GP layout.
- **Indy circuit:** the same track from start/finish through Paddock Hill, Druids, Graham Hill Bend, Cooper Straight and Surtees, then turns right at McLaren and runs Clearways and Clark Curve back to the pit straight. The GP loop leaves after Surtees down Pilgrim's Drop into the woods and rejoins before Clark Curve. MSV: Indy has pole on the right, GP has pole on the left.

## Corners
GP numbering as used by Coach Dave Academy and the oversteer48 guide (9 turns). Named features between numbered turns are listed in italics.

| T | Name | Dir | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| - | *Brabham Straight* | - | Pit/start-finish straight; pit wall on the right (inside). Slight curve about halfway along. Crest just after the start line. Road falls across its width towards the pits. |
| 1 | Paddock Hill Bend | R | Fast, blind over a crest, then drops sharply (about 8 % gradient) with a compression at the bottom; gravel run-off on the left after the dip (barriers moved back in the 1990s). Paddock Hill, Desire Wilson and Pit Straight grandstands and the Brabham-Stewart building on the outside of the approach; pit buildings opposite. Hailwoods grandstand and restaurant in the dip at the bottom. |
| - | *Hailwood Hill* | - | Steep climb from the bottom of Paddock to Druids. A **footbridge crosses the track** here, before Druids (used as braking reference). |
| 2 | Druids | R | 180-degree uphill-braking hairpin; green run-off at exit, gravel trap; red/white exit kerb then tyre barrier. Trees around it. |
| 3 | Graham Hill Bend | L | Downhill-braking, off-camber 90-degree left; big kerbs with track-limit sensors. South Bank car park overlooks it. |
| - | *Cooper Straight* | - | Runs parallel to the pit lane (behind the pits), with a kink halfway. South Bank (grass bank, car park) on the outside. |
| 4 | Surtees | L | Uphill, off-camber left, bumpy near apex. Medical Centre against the inside wall at Surtees/McLaren. On the Indy circuit it leads into McLaren (R), Clearways, Clark Curve. |
| - | *Pilgrim's Drop* | - | GP loop: long downhill, then *Hawthorn Hill* up; biggest elevation changes on the lap. Resurfaced 2023 (Surtees-Pilgrim's Drop-Hawthorn). |
| 5 | Hawthorn Bend | R | Fastest corner, ~90 degrees, gradients approaching 7 %; tight kerbs, grass beyond. Followed by *Derek Minter Straight* (name per common usage; unconfirmed in sources read). |
| 6 | Westfield Bend | R | Medium-fast, tighter than Hawthorn; generous kerbs, exit goes downhill into the Dingle Dell dip and back up. Westfield Wood around it. |
| - | *Dingle Dell* | - | Dip on the straight between Westfield and Sheene (was a chicane until 2002). |
| 7 | Sheene Curve | R | Blind, uphill, apex over a slight crest; no exit kerb or run-off, grass. Housing estate close to the track outside. |
| 8 | Stirling's | L | ~90 degrees, banked/positive camber, dip at apex. |
| - | *Clearways* | R | Short sweeping section where the GP loop rejoins; gravel/run-off reprofiled and extended for 2020; raised spectator viewing area on the outside. Main entrance nearby. |
| 9 | Clark Curve | R | Uphill, off-camber final corner onto Brabham Straight; hug the pit wall on exit. Run-off extended 2020. |

Indy circuit corners (for reference): 1 Paddock Hill Bend, 2 Druids, 3 Graham Hill Bend, 4 Surtees, 5 McLaren (R, long), 6 Clearways / Clark Curve.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** on the **inside (right in the clockwise direction)** of Brabham Straight; the pit complex sits in the Indy infield between the start/finish straight and Cooper Straight, and Cooper Straight runs parallel to the rear of the pits.
- **Pit entry:** from the Clearways/Clark Curve area on the inside (right); exact split point unconfirmed. The pit-lane entry was resurfaced in 2023.
- **Pit exit:** rejoins on the inside at the far end of the pit straight around Paddock Hill Bend (inferred from MSV trackday access instructions: from the ambulance gates at the top of Paddock Hill Bend one drives backwards along the track into the pit-lane exit). Exact merge point unconfirmed.
- **Pit building:** 34 garages with front and rear doors; control tower in the pit complex; **Nigel Mansell Centre** (also called the Media Centre) sits **above the pit complex** with near 360-degree views of the Indy circuit. Storeys unconfirmed.
- **Race control / timing:** in the control tower building of the pit complex (MSV); exact position along the pit row unconfirmed.
- **Start/finish line:** just before the crest at the end of Brabham Straight, a short distance before Paddock Hill Bend, opposite the pit buildings. Start and finish lines: not documented as separate.
- **Podium:** unconfirmed (BTCC presentations take place on the grid).
- **Paddock:** **GP Paddock** behind the garages (infield). **Upper race paddock** outside the circuit, entered from Scratcher's Lane through an industrial estate; connected to the infield/pit lane by **two tunnels under the track** (one taller and wider with a pavement, one lower). **Lower Paddock** (~22,000 m2) at the bottom of Paddock Hill with Hailwoods Restaurant and bar. Race cars/support trucks park in the inner pit-lane parking area.

## Terrain and elevation
- Natural amphitheatre (Indy circuit entirely in the bowl). Elevation change about 37 m (121 ft per lapmeta; figures range 33-59 m on that page).
- Biggest drops/climbs: Paddock Hill Bend (down ~8 %) then Hailwood Hill (steep up) to Druids; down to Graham Hill Bend; up to Surtees; Pilgrim's Drop down and Hawthorn Hill up (largest changes); dip at Dingle Dell after Westfield, uphill to Sheene; dip at Stirling's apex; Clark Curve uphill onto the straight; crest just after the start line.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Pre-2003 maps show the Dingle Dell chicane and 11 turns; the current GP has 9 and Dingle Dell is only a dip.
- Pre-1976 maps show the old Paddock Bend line and Bottom Straight.
- The GP and Indy circuits share Paddock-Druids-Graham Hill-Cooper-Surtees; the GP leaves after Surtees (Indy turns right at McLaren) and rejoins at Clearways. Do not model McLaren as part of the GP lap.
- Pole is on the left for GP races but on the right for the Indy circuit (different grid).
- Heavy elevation and camber everywhere; the straight cambers towards the pits; crest after the start line hides Paddock Hill.
- Pit lane is on the inside (right); the pit complex is an island between Brabham and Cooper Straights; tunnels, not bridges, link it to the outside paddock (the footbridge is at Hailwood Hill).
- 2020 runoff changes at Clearways and Clark Curve moved barriers outward.
