# Autódromo José Carlos Pace (Interlagos) (`Interlaggos`)

Modelled on: Interlagos, Capela do Socorro district, São Paulo, Brazil. In game: 4.30 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.309 km, 15 turns, anticlockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current configuration: the "5th variation", 1999 to present, 4.309 km, 15 turns** (Wikipedia). The F1 race is 71 laps (305.9 km).
- **1940-1979 original**: 7.960 km (Wikipedia also gives 7.874 km in its prose), 26 turns, a long outer loop around the infield. 1980-1989: 7.873 km.
- **1990 rebuild**: the circuit was cut to 4.325 km. Three long straights and nine fast corners went: five removed, four made slower. The Senna S (T1-T2) was created then. Wikipedia says the layout has been essentially unchanged since, apart from the pit exit being extended along Curva do Sol.
- 1996-1998: 4.292 km; 1999 to present: 4.309 km (small reprofiling; detail unconfirmed).
- **Pit entry**: modified in 2007 and given a chicane in the pit-entry lane for 2014 (the racing line itself is unchanged).
- **Other variants**: a Stock Car layout with a chicane (2011-2017, 4.314 km, 17 turns) and a 1992 motorcycle circuit (4.352 km, 17 turns). Neither is the F1 track.
- **In-game 4.30 km matches the current 1999+ F1 layout (4.309 km).**

## Corners

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Main straight ("Reta de Largada", Tribunas / "Emerson Fittipaldi" straight)**: rises uphill to the crest before T1. Grandstands A, B and M are on the outside, the right-hand side when driving. The pit lane and pit building are on the **inside, the left-hand side**. Grandstand B sits opposite the team garages and the podium.
- **Pit entry**: on the left (inside) of the climb up Subida dos Boxes, before Arquibancadas (T15), through a chicane built in the pit-entry lane for 2014.
- **Pit lane**: one of the longest in F1. It runs the full length of the start straight on the inside and continues past T1-T2.
- **Pit exit**: runs inside the Senna S and rejoins **on the left (inside) along Curva do Sol (T3)**, directly in front of Grandstand R. This exit lane was extended along Curva do Sol in the 1990 rebuild.
- **Pit building**: it holds the team garages, press centre and VIP area. A 2015 report said the old building would be demolished and replaced by a taller building with wider garages (28 boxes, five more than before). A team building with the Paddock Club on its upper floor, above the boxes, was due in 2016. Exact storeys are unconfirmed. Today the **F1 Paddock Club sits above the pit lane** (gpdestinations).
- **Control tower / operations**: a six-storey operations-centre building was built in 2015 (tnh1, citing the works). Its exact position in the pit complex is unconfirmed.
- **Paddock**: behind the pit garages, on the inside of the main straight. It was widened by about 2 m in 2015.
- **Hospitality Center (2025)**: a new building behind the boxes, built for the 2025 GP. Reports give 22,000 m² for up to 6,000 people (Diário de Pernambuco). Band reverses the two numbers: about 6,000 m² for up to 22,000 people.
- **Podium**: on the pit side, facing Grandstand B. Sector B and the Pit Stop Club are sold on their view of the grid, pits and podium.
- **Start and finish line**: on the main straight, which the F1 site calls a half-oval with banked corners. A different start line is not documented.
- **Support paddock**: location unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
- The venue sits about **800 m above sea level** (Mercedes).
- The track forms a natural bowl, with **43 m of elevation change** (F1).
- **High point**: the crest between the start/finish line and T1. From there the track "plunges downhill all the way to Turn 5, the lowest point" (F1). Mercedes gives the largest drop as about 40 m, start straight to T4.
- The infield (T6-T11) climbs and falls with camber changes.
- From the exit of T12 (Junção) to the T1 braking zone the track climbs **33 m** over about 1.2 km flat-out. Subida dos Boxes runs at about 10%.

## Key coordinates
- Centre of circuit: **23.70111 S, 46.69722 W** (Wikipedia).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: not confirmed here. Read them from OSM relation 2560355. The pit lane follows the inside of the main straight; the pit exit is on Curva do Sol.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **Pit exit length**: the pit lane does not end at T1. It continues inside the Senna S and rejoins on Curva do Sol (T3), on the left. Models that end the pit lane at the start-line gantry are wrong.
- **Pit entry**: the entry is on the uphill left before Arquibancadas, with a **chicane in the pit-entry lane** (2014). Older maps show a straight entry.
- **Gradients matter**: the start straight is uphill to a crest, the track drops about 40 m to T4-T5, and it climbs 33 m back up through Junção, Café and Subida dos Boxes. A flat model loses the character of the track.
- **Banking**: the final curve (Arquibancadas) and the start "half-oval" are banked.
- **Pre-1990 maps**: they show the 7.9 km outer loop, which is gone. Some 1990s maps show the earlier pit exit before it was extended along Curva do Sol.
- **Grandstand letters change year to year**: Q became G, and V, S and N appear only in older guides. Use the 2026 official map.
- **Not to be confused**: the 2011-2017 Stock Car chicane layout (17 turns).
- **Temporary buildings**: the Hospitality Center and the Paris modular stands are recent (2025). The 2025 service tunnel under the Reta Oposta is new and is not in older aerial imagery.
