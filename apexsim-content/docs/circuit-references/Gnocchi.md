# Sochi Autodrom (`Gnocchi`)

Modelled on: Sochi Autodrom (renamed **Sirius Autodrom** on 1 April 2024). It sits in the 2014 Winter Olympic Park, Imeretinsky Valley, Sirius federal territory, Krasnodar Krai, Russia, on the Black Sea coast about 33 km from Sochi near Sochi (Adler) airport. In game: 5.83 km, F1. Real (last F1 configuration, 2014-2021): 5.848 km, 18 turns (12 right, 6 left), clockwise. **The venue is defunct for F1** and its GP loop has been dismantled.

## Which layout
- **Grand Prix layout, 2014-2021: 5.848 km, 18 turns, clockwise** (FIA, F1). Hermann Tilke designed it.
  - It has a **2.7 km permanent section**: the pit straight, T1 and the final sector.
  - The rest is a **temporary section through the Olympic Park**, whose roads are public traffic and pedestrian zones when there is no racing.
  - It hosted the Russian GP from 2014 to 2021, 53 laps. F1 terminated the contract before the 2022 race after the invasion of Ukraine.
  - Other series: F2/GP2, F3/GP3, WTCR and TCR.
- **Same layout throughout 2014-2021.**
  - Turn 2 had its kerbs, run-off and "return route" changed over the years, with an array of blocks to rejoin around before T3 (FIA event notes 2021). Its apex was resurfaced in 2021.
  - The T1 kerbs were replaced for 2018.
  - Some game and media guides count 19 turns; the FIA counts 18.
- **Start and finish lines differ.** The 2014 FIA preview gives a start/finish line offset of 0.212 km: race distance 309.732 km = 53 x 5.848 minus 0.212. By 2021 F1 gave 309.745 km, which implies about 0.199 km. The start line is ahead of the finish line on the pit straight; the exact positions are unconfirmed.
- **2023 dismantling.** After the November 2023 Russian Endurance Cup, the GP loop was dismantled. **Only the 2.313 km short layout remains (11 turns), with T1 linked straight to T13.** It has been in use since December 2023 (SMP F4 Winter Series and others).
- **In-game 5.83 km matches the 2014-2021 GP layout (5.848 km).** Model that layout, not the current 2.3 km circuit, and use 2014-2021 maps and imagery.

## Corners
Directions confirmed by sources are marked. The others are **unconfirmed** and need checking against the F1 map. The total must come to 12 right and 6 left.

| T | Name | Dir | Notes |

| 3 | - | L | 750 m constant-radius (multi-apex, decreasing) left, **wrapping around the Medals Plaza** and its fountain (Poyushchiye / "singing" fountain). Adverse camber, above 300 km/h. T3 "Daniil Kvyat" grandstand on the outside. |
| 4 | - | R | 90-degree right after T3, an overtaking spot. T4 grandstand on the outside. Its braking zone was shortened (RaceFans). General admission from here around the Bolshoy Ice Dome. |
| 5-9 | - | mix (unconfirmed) | "A series of similar medium-to-low-speed right-left turns" (RaceFans), including **three turns around the Bolshoy Ice Dome** (Wikipedia). The GA zone surrounds the Dome and sees T3/T4-T9. The T8 surface was improved for 2018. |

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit straight**: on the northern edge of the Olympic Park next to the **Olympic Park railway station**. It heads south-west towards the Black Sea coast.
  - The **pit lane and pit building are on the right-hand side**. The FIA notes say "keep to the right of the white line" at pit entry and exit, and the white line on the left edge of the pit entry separates it from the track.
  - The **Main Grandstand is on the left**, opposite the pit building.
- **Pit entry**: on the right, **between T17 and T18**, with a bollard at the entry.
- **Pit exit**: on the right after the pit building. A solid white pit-exit line must not be crossed, and the dotted line across the exit marks the track edge. It rejoins towards T1; the exact merge point is unconfirmed.
- **Pit lane**: speed limit 80 km/h in 2014 and 60 km/h from 2017. Time loss about 22 s. Length unconfirmed.
- **Pit building**: permanent, with **33 team garages (6.80 m x 17.65 m each)**, FIA and FOM garages, and Paddock Club VIP suites with outside seating on the upper level. The **Media Centre is on the first floor** (168-seat press room). Storey count and length unconfirmed.
- **Team buildings (paddock)**: **16 permanent two-storey team buildings opposite the garages**, i.e. behind the pit building, across the paddock lane. Hospitality and technical rooms are on the ground floor; offices, driver rooms and a terrace on the first floor.
- **Race Control Tower**: a separate building housing race control, the race director, the FOM and FIA offices and timing; position unconfirmed. Its video control system had 56 screens fed by 45 trackside cameras.
- **Medical Centre** (ICU and two surgery rooms) and a **30 x 30 m helipad**: in the permanent section; positions unconfirmed.
- **Media Accreditation Centre**: near the Olympic Park and Imeretinskiy Kurort railway stops.
- **Podium**: position unconfirmed; most likely on the pit building facing the Main Grandstand.

## Terrain and elevation
- **Essentially flat**: altitude 21.4 to 23.3 m, a range of only **1.9 m** (FIA, F1).
- The approach to the station behind the paddock "climbs" slightly (Wikipedia). There are no significant gradients.

## Key coordinates
- Circuit: **43.4083 N, 39.9656 E** (43°24′30″N 39°57′56″E, ru.wikipedia).
- RaceFans map embed centre: 43.40494 N, 39.96320 E.
- Olympic Park centre: about 43.4056 N, 39.9547 E (43°24′20″N 39°57′17″E, Wikipedia).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: unconfirmed. The pit straight is on the northern edge of the park by the railway station, and the pit entry is between T17 and T18.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **The current OSM data and satellite imagery show the 2.313 km short layout** (T1 linked to T13). The Olympic Park loop (T2-T12) is no longer a circuit. Use 2014-2021 imagery and F1 or FIA maps for the 5.848 km layout.
- **Start and finish lines differ**: the grid is about 0.2 km away from the finish line on the pit straight.
- **The pit lane is on the right.** Pit entry is between T17 and T18, not after T18.
- **T3 is a 750 m left at more than 300 km/h**, not a hairpin. It wraps around the Medals Plaza and its fountain, with adverse camber.
- **T2**: modern maps show a wide asphalt run-off with a rejoin block array. Early (2014) imagery differs.
- **Turn count**: 18 (FIA). Some games and guides number 19.
- **Temporary and permanent features**: four temporary grandstands at T1-T4 and a permanent 400 m, six-storey Main Grandstand. The track through the park is public road and plaza, with the Olympic venues forming the backdrop.
- **Trademarks**: Olympic venue names (Fisht, Bolshoy, Iceberg and others) are real names. They belong in `name`, not on screen.
