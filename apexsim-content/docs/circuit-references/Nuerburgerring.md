# Nürburgring GP-Strecke, Germany (`Nuerburgerring`)

Modelled on: Nürburg, Rhineland-Palatinate (Eifel), Germany. In game: 5.14 km, DTM. Real (current layout): 5.148 km, 15 turns, clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current GP-Strecke (2002 to date): 5.148 km, 15 turns, clockwise.** The 1984 circuit (4.556 km incl. the 1995 F1 chicane) was extended for 2002 by the Tilke-designed **Mercedes-Arena**, an omega-shaped infield section built on the former kart track next to the paddock, which replaced the old "Castrol" chicane at the end of the straight with a sharp right-hander (nicknamed "Haug-Haken"). The F1 chicane at the end of the back straight (Veedol/NGK-Schikane) was revised to its current Z shape for 2003. Lengths per racingcircuits.info: 2003-to-date with arena + F1 chicane 5.148 km; without the arena 4.579 km.
- **The in-game 5.14 km matches the full 5.148 km GP layout with the Mercedes-Arena and the F1/Veedol chicane.** This is the layout DTM races.
- Earlier: 1984-89 GP 4.551 km; 1995-97 with the F1 chicane 4.556 km (racingcircuits.info). The GP circuit shares only the start/finish straight area with the pre-1984 Start-und-Ziel-Schleife (de.wikipedia).
- **Sprintstrecke (2003 to date): 3.629 km (with arena and F1 chicane), 12 turns**; 3.059 km without the arena. It uses the "Kurzanbindung" (short link, since 2002) and skips the southern **Müllenbachschleife** (Dunlop-Kehre, Michael-Schumacher-S, Kumho-Kurve area). The exact junction points of the Kurzanbindung are inferred from the length difference (5.148 - 3.629 = 1.519 km, close to the 1.489 km loop), not confirmed from a drawing.
- **Müllenbachschleife: 1.489 km** stand-alone loop in the southern part of the GP circuit, created 1990 with a link road just south of the Kurzanbindung; used for testing, club events and the Truck Grand Prix camping area; no pits of its own.
- **24h combination (Nordschleife + GP without the Mercedes-Arena): 25.378 km**, 170 turns (en.wikipedia). The NLS (VLN) combination without the Müllenbachschleife is 24.433 km (de.wikipedia). The combined layouts use the GP pit complex.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane and pit building** are on the infield side of the start/finish straight, i.e. on the drivers' right (clockwise lap); the main grandstand T3 is "gegenüber den Boxen" (opposite the pits, de.wikipedia). The pit building was rebuilt completely in 2000 with **33 garages** (de.wikipedia); storeys unconfirmed (multi-storey with lounges/VIP above the garages: business°lounge and Panorama-Lounges are named venues, positions unconfirmed).
- **Pit entry:** on the inside of the Coca-Cola-Kurve (T15) (Coach Dave cites the pit wall at T15). Exact split point unconfirmed.
- **Pit exit:** at the end of the straight toward T1 on the inside; whether it rejoins before or after T1 is unconfirmed.
- **Start vs finish:** the FIA 2020 event data give a start/finish line offset of 0.263 km (start line and control line 263 m apart); which comes first is not stated in the text extract. Pole is on the left (f1-fansite).
- **Paddock:** behind the pit building, enclosed by the Mercedes-Arena (the arena was built on the former kart track next to the paddock). The DTM Fan Zone sits in the centre of the paddock; a PSCCD (Porsche Carrera Cup) support paddock is named; Historic Paddock (Historisches Fahrerlager) is the old 1927 paddock area near the Nordschleife side (position unconfirmed). A pedestrian tunnel links the ring°boulevard (outside the straight) to the paddock.
- **Race control / timing tower, media centre, podium:** in the pit/paddock complex; exact positions unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
- Around 600 m above sea level on a plateau; GP circuit elevation change about 56 m (f1-fansite). The lowest point is the Dunlop-Kehre (T7) in the south; the track climbs back through the Schumacher-S and Kumho-Kurve and drops again into the Bit-Kurve. The Mercedes-Arena descends from T1 through T3.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Old maps (1984-2001) show the Castrol chicane at the end of the straight and no Mercedes-Arena; 2002 maps show the earlier F1 chicane shape. The current Veedol-Schikane is the 2003 Z shape.
- Corner names are sponsor names and change (Castrol-S/Yokohama-S, RTL-/Kumho-/Warsteiner-Kurve, NGK-/Veedol-Schikane, Shell-S/Michael-Schumacher-S).
- The no-arena runoff/bypass road from T1 to the Valvoline-Kurve exists and is used by the 24h and NLS layouts; it must not be drawn as the main line.
- The Kurzanbindung (Sprint) and the Müllenbach link road are real tarmac connections inside the southern part of the circuit.
- There is a current bridge over the start/finish straight (BMW M Bridge) plus visitor tunnels; an older footbridge was removed around 2009, so old photos show a different structure.
- Grandstand numbering is not continuous around the lap in a simple way (T5a-T10b in the south, T12 on the back straight, T13 at the Nordschleife junction).
