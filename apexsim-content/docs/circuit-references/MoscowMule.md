# Moscow Raceway (`MoscowMule`)

Modelled on: Moscow Raceway, near Sheludkovo / Chismena, Volokolamsk district, Moscow Oblast, Russia (~95 km west of Moscow, off the M9 Novorizhskoe highway at km 95). In game: 4.05 km, DTM. Real: 21 official configurations, all **anticlockwise**; designed full Grand Prix length 4.070 km (official "Гран При 2", 19 turns; Wikipedia lists the full circuit as 21 turns); DTM's official layout "Гран При 9" 3.931 km, 13 turns; FIM (motorcycle) 3.932 km, 15 turns.

Status: not defunct. The venue still runs Russian national series (RCRS, SMP F4, RSKG, TCR Russia in 2025). International events stopped after 2017-2019 (DTM 2013-2017). Its FIA Grade 1 licence (GP 1 layout) expired on 3 July 2024.

## Which layout
- **Opened** 13/15 July 2012 (World Series by Renault). Designed by Hermann Tilke for FIA 1T and FIM A; inspected 18 July 2012 and graded FIM B. Track width 12-21 m, start straight 15 m wide; longest straight 873-879 m (the back straight leading to the last corner).

- **Which one the game matches:** 4.05 km does not match any official configuration exactly. It is closest to **GP 2, the full 4.070 km circuit** (the length quoted for "the Grand Prix track" during construction), 20 m longer than the game. The next nearest are GP 3 (4.012 km) and GP 4 (4.009 km). The DTM never raced it: DTM used Sprint 4 (2.555 km) in 2013 and GP 9 (3.931 km) in 2014-2017. FIM (3.932 km, 15 turns) and GP 9 (3.931 km, 13 turns) are near-identical in length but differ in turn count: they take different infield/chicane options. **If the game is meant to be the DTM track, it should be 3.931 km (GP 9); if it is meant to be the full circuit, 4.070 km (GP 2).** Which sections each configuration includes could not be read from text; use the racingcircuits.info diagrams below.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Start/finish:** on the main straight; pole position is on the **right for a standing start** and on the **left for a rolling start** (all configurations except SuperSprint).
- **Pit lane / pit building:** a pit building with pit boxes ("Пит-боксы") along the start/finish straight; side of the straight, length, number of boxes and storeys unconfirmed. VIP boxes and a **rooftop terrace** (ВИП-ложи, терраса на крыше) are listed with the grandstands, most likely on the pit building (unconfirmed).
- **Pit entry / exit:** unconfirmed.
- **Race control, media centre, podium:** unconfirmed.
- **Paddock:** 52,000 m2. Reached through an **automobile tunnel** (vehicle entry to the paddock) and a **pedestrian tunnel** (pedestrian entry to the paddock) under the track; positions unconfirmed.
- **Medical centre:** on site (built with the pit building); position unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
Elevation difference 22 m; maximum climb 4 degrees, maximum descent 4.5 degrees (4 degrees on GP 11). Start/finish at 22 m (Wikipedia: relative datum). Fairly gentle terrain in forest clearing.

## Modelling pitfalls
- 21 configurations share one site; layout diagrams online often mislabel them (racingcircuits.info's "FIM" 3.931 km map vs the official GP 9 3.931 km / FIM 3.932 km). Decide which configuration the game should be: the DTM raced GP 9 (3.931 km, 13 turns) from 2014 and Sprint 4 (2.555 km) in 2013; the game's 4.05 km is near the full GP 2 (4.070 km, 19 turns).
- All configurations are anticlockwise.
- Grid: pole on the right for standing starts, left for rolling starts.
- Paddock access is by tunnels under the track, not bridges.
- The venue is still operating (not abandoned), but without FIA Grade 1 since July 2024 and without international series since the late 2010s; use 2014-2017 DTM-era materials for the DTM look.
