# Sepang International Circuit (`Shebang`)

Modelled on: Sepang, Selangor, Malaysia, next to Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA), about 45 km south of Kuala Lumpur. In game: 5.53 km, F1, with a double-sided main grandstand. Real (current layout): 5.543 km, 15 turns, clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Grand Prix Circuit, 1999 to present: 5.543 km, 15 turns, clockwise.** Hermann Tilke designed it; it was completed late 1998 and opened on 7 March 1999. It hosted the Malaysian GP from 1999 to 2017, and it hosts the MotoGP Malaysian GP and WorldSBK.
- **F1 returned in 2026.** The **"Formula 1 Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia"** was run at Sepang on **2-4 October 2026** (56 laps, 310.4 km). For that event the FIA moved the T15 pit-entry bollard further back, the night before FP3.
- **2016 resurfacing (Dromo)**: the corner sequence did not change, but nine corners were re-profiled.
  - **T15**: the inside was raised about 1 m. Its camber is now -4% (reverse) against +2% at the apex, and the corner rises towards its exit.
  - **T2**: the inside was raised to remove a dip.
  - **T5**: the inside banking was re-profiled.
  - **T3, T4, T6, T9, T12, T13**: gradients changed.
  - **T1 and T4**: approach bumps removed.
  - 600 m of kerbs were rebuilt and the run-offs updated.
- **Variants**: the North Circuit (2.71-2.81 km; uses T1-T6, then cuts back to the pit straight) and the South Circuit (2.609 km; the back straight becomes its pit straight). The two can run at the same time.
- **In-game 5.53 km matches the GP circuit (5.543 km).** No other configuration is close.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Main straight**: runs east to west (the official site says the grandstand is "along the east-west alignment"). T15 is at the east end and T1 at the west end.
  - The **pit lane and pit building are on the north side, the right-hand side when driving**, facing the Main Grandstand North across the track.
  - The **back straight** runs parallel on the south side of the grandstand, travelling west to east.
- **Pit entry**: on the right, at the exit of the T15 hairpin. A bollard at the track edge marks it. In 2026 the FIA moved the bollard back so that a car running wide at T15 can stay on track.
- **Pit exit**: at the west end, towards T1-T2. K1 overlooks "the pitlane exit and the pit exit area toward Turn 4". The exact merge point is unconfirmed; check the F1 2026 map.
- **Pit lane**: 420 m (SportsKeeda 2013); time loss about 19 s.
- **Pit building**:
  - It is "the heart and focal point of the circuit". The official site gives **33 pits**; Racing Circuits Info gives 30.
  - It houses race control, timekeeping and offices. Racing Circuits Info lists the media centre, interview room, photographers' room, FIA offices and a medical centre.
  - Official storey layout: paddock clubs on the **1st floor**, the **Perdana Suite** (250 people) on the **2nd floor**, and a **rooftop terrace along the whole building** on the 3rd floor. Racing Circuits Info says two storeys.
- **Paddock**: behind (north of) the pit building, with the **Paddock Chalets**: 14 chalets in two rows. A tunnel takes drivers straight to the paddock (a 2017 comment on gpdestinations).
- **South Paddock**: a separate two-storey, dual-frontage modular building, used as the support paddock for the South Circuit. Its position is on the SIC map (p.3); unconfirmed here.
- **Podium**: above the pit lane, opposite the Main Grandstand North. North Main Grandstand tickets are sold on their view of the grid, garages and podium.
- **Start and finish line**: on the main straight, opposite the Diamond/Crystal sections and pit boxes 1-7.

## Terrain and elevation
- **22 m** of elevation difference between T3 and T11 (F1), with several smaller rises and falls.
- T2 runs significantly downhill.
- The exit of the T9 hairpin climbs.
- T15 rises towards its exit since 2016.
- The two straights and the grandstand area are essentially level.

## Key coordinates
- Circuit: **2.7606 N, 101.7375 E** (2°45′38″N 101°44′15″E, Wikipedia).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: not measured here. Read them from OSM or aerial imagery. The pit entry is at the T15 exit (east end of the main straight) and the pit exit at the west end.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **The grandstand sits between the straights, not beside them.** The Main Grandstand is one double-faced structure between the main and back straights, V-shaped and converging at the T15 hairpin (Tower). The pit building is on the opposite (north) side of the main straight.
- **The straights are not parallel**: they converge at T15 into a V. They are not a hairpin between two parallel lines.
- **T15 since 2016**: reverse camber (-4%), the inside raised by 1 m, and the corner climbs to its exit. Pre-2016 track models have a flat T15.
- **2026 pit-entry bollard**: it was moved back at T15 during the 2026 event. Before the move, running wide forced cars into the pit lane.
- **Corner numbering**: some old guides and MotoGP materials call the final hairpin "Turn 13". Some previews call T7-T8 lefts; they are rights.
- **The F1 race name changed**: 2026 is the "Bahrain GP in Malaysia". Use the GP circuit (5.543 km), not the North or South loops.
- **Pit-building figures differ**: 30 or 33 pits, two or three floors plus a rooftop. Use the official SIC figure of 33 and treat the rest as approximate.
