# Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya (`Barcelunatic`)

Modelled on: Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, Montmeló, Vallès Oriental, about 32 km north of Barcelona, Spain. In game: 4.64 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.657 km, 14 turns (8 right, 6 left), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current F1 configuration (since 2023): 4.657 km, 14 turns, no chicane before the final corner.** The 2007 RACC chicane (old T14-T15) is bypassed, so the old T13 and New Holland corners form two fast right-handers (new T13, T14) onto the straight.
  - It also carries the **2021 reprofiled T10 (La Caixa)**, which is wider and faster than the 2004-2020 hairpin.
  - The main straight is about 1.05 km.
- Layout history (Wikipedia; Tracing Insights):

  | Years | Length | Turns | What changed |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1991-1993 | 4.747 km | 14 | Original |
  | 1994 | 4.745 km | 16 | Temporary Nissan chicane |
  | 1995-2003 | 4.730 km | 13 | |
  | 2004-2006 | 4.627 km | 14 | New, slower La Caixa (T10) |
  | 2007-2020 | 4.655 km | 16 | Final fast sweeper replaced by the slow RACC chicane (T14-T15), New Holland as T16 |
  | 2021-2022 | 4.675 km | 16 | T10 reprofiled, chicane kept |
  | 2023-now | 4.657 km | 14 | Chicane dropped for F1 |

- **Which one the game matches: the length alone cannot tell.**
  - 4.64 km is about 15-17 m short of both the 2007-2020 chicane layout (4.655) and the current no-chicane layout (4.657), and 13 m longer than 2004-2006 (4.627).
  - Check the geometry instead:
    - A tight right-left chicane before the last corner means a 2007-2022 layout.
    - A tight La Caixa hairpin as well means 2007-2020.
  - **The 2026 F1 layout has no chicane and the 2021 T10.** The brief's suspicion of an old layout fits a model with the chicane, and its 16 turns would be the tell.
- **Variants:**
  - The chicane layout (4.675 km) remains available.
  - MotoGP historically used different T10 and final-sector versions.
  - National circuit: 2.977 km.
- **Event:** the 2026 race was the "Gran Premio de Barcelona-Catalunya" (14 June 2026); the Spanish GP moved to Madrid. Barcelona has a rotating deal for 2028, 2030 and 2032.
- **2026 changes** (FIA notes):
  - New asphalt along the pit-lane working lane.
  - T13 left-side asphalt beyond the green area replaced by gravel.
  - New blue lines outside the white lines at T1, T2, T3, T8, T10 and T13.
  - Barriers moved back at T5 entry (left) and T6 (right).
  - New tyre barrier on the right of the pit-entry road.
  - Escape-road bollards at T1 removed.

## Corners
- **Names:** Wikipedia gives T1 Elf, T3 Renault, T4 Repsol, T5 Seat, T9 Campsa, T10 La Caixa. Würth, Europcar, RACC and New Holland come from the grandstand guides and are placed by their descriptions.
- **Directions:** Tracing Insights confirms the total of 8 right and 6 left, and that T3, T9, T13 and T14 are right and T5 and T10 are left. **The directions of T2, T4, T6, T7, T8, T11 and T12 are inferred from the standard layout and are unconfirmed.**

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** the pit lane is on the **right** of the main straight (inside), and the Main Grandstand (Principal / D) is on the left.
  - FIA 2026 notes: pit entry is to the right of the bollard; practice-start boxes are on the right at pit exit; grid panels are on the right; the yellow light panel is on the left of the pit-entry road.
- **Pit entry:** on the right after the final corner (T14). A new tyre barrier lines the right of the entry road (2026).
- **Pit exit:** a **long pit-exit road on the right that continues alongside the track through T1**.
  - Practice-start cars must rejoin after T1 and never cross the white line.
  - Other cars cross the line to the left at the first chance and take T1 normally.
  - Practice starts are held near the 100 m board on the right.
- **Start / finish:** the race finishes at the Control Line on the track. The grid has 30 boxes, with grid-removal gates at positions 10 and 21. The start-line offset is not documented in the sources used. Main Grandstand sections 1-10 are nearer pit entry and the podium; 11-28 are nearer the start/finish line and pit exit.
- **Pit building:** right side of the straight.
  - The Montjuïc Club is on the first floor above the pits.
  - The **F1 Paddock Club** is on the roof / upper level of the pit building.
  - The "Pitlane Lounge" is inside near the pit exit.
  - The podium is on the pit building, on the pit-entry half of the straight (unconfirmed).
  - Storeys and length are unconfirmed.
- **Control / scoreboard tower:** on the pit side near the pit exit, opposite stand J (Grand Prix Grand Tours). The race control position is unconfirmed.
- **Paddock:** behind the pit building (inside the circuit), "F1 Paddock" on the 2026 map. A VIP stand at the back of the paddock is reached by a pedestrian bridge and overlooks T1 (reader comment, unconfirmed).
- **Media centre:** labelled "Media Centre" on the 2026 spectator map. Position unconfirmed.
- **F1 Experiences Champions Club:** a newer building on the inside near the pit exit, with a roof terrace.

## Terrain and elevation
- **Elevation change:** 29.6 m (F1.com).
- **Lowest:** at T1.
- **Highest:** around T12 (2016 numbering, the same as today's T12).
- The main straight runs downhill to T1, then the track climbs through T3 (Renault).
- T5 is a downhill left, and T7-T8 climb again.
- T9 Campsa is a blind crest.
- The back straight falls to T10. The stadium section and the final corners lead back onto the straight.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **The chicane (old T14-T15, RACC) is not used by F1 since 2023.** Maps from 2007-2022 show 16 turns; current F1 maps show 14.
  - The chicane tarmac and kerbs still physically exist, so model them as a bypassed section, not as missing.
- **T10 La Caixa changed in 2021.** It is wider and faster than the 2004-2020 tight hairpin. Older maps show the old shape, and 1991-2003 maps show an even faster original.
- **The pit exit continues through T1** as a separate lane on the right. It is not a short merge on the straight.
- **The pit building is on the right**, and the permanent covered Main Grandstand on the left.
- **The length does not identify the layout** (4.655 vs 4.657 km). Use the corner count and the shapes.
- **Turn numbering differs on older maps** (16 turns with the chicane). Grandstand guides written before 2023 reference the "final chicane".
