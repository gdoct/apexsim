# Circuit Zandvoort (`Zandervoort`)

Modelled on: Circuit Zandvoort (CM.com / "MASCOT" Circuit Zandvoort), in the dunes just north-east of the seaside town of Zandvoort, North Holland, Netherlands, ~35 km west of Amsterdam. In game: 4.31 km, GT3 (DTM). Real (current layout): 4.259 km, 14 turns, clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current: 2020-present, 4.259 km, 14 turns.** Rebuilt over winter 2019-20 for F1's return (reopened 4 March 2020; first F1 race in 2021 after the 2020 GP was cancelled). Changes: Hugenholtzbocht (T3) re-profiled with progressive banking up to 18 degrees and moved ~16 m away from the infield (east) to make room for a bigger paddock; Arie Luyendykbocht (T14, formerly "Bos Uit") banked 15-18 degrees (max slope 32%), the T13-T14 pair now being a banked "combination corner"; Tarzanbocht exit widened by 2 m and the **pit exit moved to the exit of Tarzan**; Gerlachbocht widened; gravel at Tarzan, Hans Ernst and Masters replaced/supplemented by asphalt run-off; two new tunnels under the track; new embankment along the straight for temporary grandstands. Turn names: T10 was "Vodafonebocht"/"Bocht Zonder Naam" (now also "CM.com bocht"), T11-12 "Audi S"/"Nissanbocht" (now Hans Ernst chicane), T13 "Kumhobocht", T8 "Marlborobocht" (now Mastersbocht), T9 "Renaultbocht".
- **1999/2001-2019: 4.307 km, 15 turns** ("international" GP circuit restored after the 1990s club era; new pit building and main grandstand built ~2001). Same route as today but with the old, flatter Hugenholtz (further west, tighter) and flat Bos Uit / final corner, and the pit exit on the straight before Tarzan.
- 1990-1998: 2.526 km "club circuit" (southern section sold off for housing, the Vendorado bungalow park and sports fields).
- 1980-1989: 4.252 km; 1972-1979: 4.226 km (chicanes added at what are now T8 and T11; Panoramabocht 1973); 1948-1971: 4.193 km original (corners Hoek van Tarzan, Hunserug, Scheivlak, Bos In, Bos Uit; also the old "Tunnel Oost" section).
- **In-game 4.31 km matches the 1999-2019 layout (4.307 km), not the current 4.259 km.** If the game claims the current F1/DTM track, T3 and T14 geometry (banking, Hugenholtz position) and the pit exit are the pre-2020 versions.
- Variants: F1, F1 Academy, Porsche Supercup, DTM, GT World Challenge, GT4 European Series all use the same 4.259 km GP layout (GT4 briefing gives 4259 m). The 2.5 km club circuit no longer exists as a separate configuration (unconfirmed whether any shortcut is still raceable).

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Main straight** runs roughly SSW to NNE on the west side of the site; cars run north toward Tarzan. **Pit lane and pit building are on the right (east, inside) of the straight**; grandstands 26-29 on the left (west).
- **Pit entry:** on the right just after the exit of T14 (drivers passing right of the bollard at Safety Car Line 1 are deemed to be entering, FIA 2025 event notes).
- **Pit exit:** since 2020 rejoins on the inside (right) at the **exit of Tarzanbocht (T1)**, before Gerlach, via a pit exit road separated from the track by a white line; cars must cross it at the earliest opportunity before the dashed line. Pit exit lights show a warning if there is an obstacle "around the corner".
- **Pit lane** is notoriously narrow. Speed limit 60 km/h for F1 until 2024, raised to 80 km/h from 2025 (50 km/h in GT/GT4). GT4 briefing gives "Pit IN to Pit OUT: 385 m"; another source quotes 730 m; length unconfirmed.
- **Start line is 56 m beyond the Control (finish/timing) line**; pole on the right (GT4 briefing). Finish line roughly level with the Main Grandstand (28) per the chequered-flag icon on the 2026 map.
- **Pit building:** built ~2001, facelifted 2020; along the east side of the straight. Media Centre on its **1st floor** (FIA media guide), at the south end of the block on the 2026 map. F1 Paddock Club (red on map) along/above the pit building; Royal Lounge (22) at its north end. Storeys and length unconfirmed. Race control and podium location unconfirmed.
- **F1 paddock:** the infield between the pit building and T2/T3, extending south inside T13/T14 (green hatch on the 2026 map); enlarged in 2020 by moving T3 east. Connected to grid power.
- **Support paddock (F1 Academy & Porsche Supercup):** outside the circuit, west of the south end of the straight, beside the N200 and roundabout (2026 map). Marktlink House (20) next to it.

## Terrain and elevation
Built in sand dunes, partly below sea level. Elevation range quoted as ~8.9 m (Wikipedia; probably understated for the dunes). Main features: Hugenholtz banking (~4.5 m inside-to-outside), the crest at Hunserug (T4), the climb through T5-T6 to the dune top where Scheivlak (T7) starts and drops downhill, and the T13-T14 banking onto the straight. The main straight is roughly flat.

- Circuit centre: 52.38889 N, 4.54083 E (Wikipedia, 52°23'20"N 4°32'27"E).

## Modelling pitfalls
- **In-game length 4.31 km = the pre-2020 layout.** The current track is 4.259 km with banked T3 (moved east) and banked T13-T14.
- Pit exit rejoins at **T1 exit** since 2020 (not on the straight); pit entry after T14 on the right.
- Start line is 56 m after the finish/control line.
- Corner numbering for T4-T7 differs between sources (circuit site: T5 Slotemaker, T6/T7 Scheivlak; Wikipedia: T5 nameless, T6 Slotemaker; 2026 map: Hunserug, Slotemakerbocht, Bocht 6, Scheivlak). Old names (Marlboro, Renault, Vodafone, Audi S, Nissan, Kumho, Bos Uit) appear on older maps.
- Most grandstands (except the permanent main/pit stands) are **temporary**, as are the fanzone, campsite and many lounges: ~85,000 temporary seats. GA dune banks are permanent landforms.
- The track is very narrow; run-off is a mix of gravel, asphalt and escape roads (T1, T8, T11 escape roads).
- The support paddock is outside the circuit (west), not in the infield.
