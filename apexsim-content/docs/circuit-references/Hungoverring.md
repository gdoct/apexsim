# Hungaroring (`Hungoverring`)

Modelled on: Hungaroring, Mogyoród, Pest County, Hungary (about 20 km north-east of Budapest, beside the M3 motorway, exit 20). In game: 4.37 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.381 km, 14 turns (8 right, 6 left), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current configuration: 4.381 km since 2003.** The in-game 4.37 km matches it, not the 1989-2002 3.975 km layout.
- History (Wikipedia; hungaroring.hu history page):
  - 1986-1988: 4.014 km, 16 turns. A chicane after T3 avoided a spring (hence the T3 name "Spring").
  - 1989: the stream was culverted and the chicane removed, giving 3.975 km.
  - 2003: the main straight was lengthened by about 200 m to 908 m, the T1 hairpin tightened, and T12 (and T14, per the circuit) altered. This gave 4.381 km.
  - 2016: fully resurfaced.
  - 2024: start/finish straight resurfaced after the tunnel work.
  - 2025: main straight and pit lane resurfaced, T5 exit grass replaced by gravel, new concrete blocks and fencing on the left of T14 "up to the bridge", and the T7 exit white line realigned.
  - 2026: asphalt repairs on the approaches to T1 and T12. More debris fencing at T3, T11 and T14 (Xpatloop, July 2026).
- **2024-2026 redevelopment (the big change for modellers):**
  - Phase 1 (2024): paddock widened to 8,000 m² after the low depot buildings were demolished; two tunnels under the start/finish straight; new fan-zone terraces behind the main grandstand with 450 m of retaining walls.
  - Phase 2 (after the 2024 race): **new pit building**, unveiled for the 2025 GP and built in nine months.
    - 36 race garages and 4 technical garages.
    - A 2,000-person VIP area over the pit lane.
    - Press conference room, offices, rooftop terrace.
  - **New covered main grandstand**: 10,000 seats, 13,000 m², commentary booths.
  - **2026 (40th anniversary):** the main building is fully finished and inaugurated, with race control and the media centre now permanent (temporary in 2025). The Paddock Club grew from 950 to more than 3,800 seats.
  - About USD 70 million in total.
- **Corner names:** all corners got official names for the first time in 2026 (see the table below).
- **Grandstand names:** these changed around 2025. Old names (Super Gold, Gold 1-4, Silver 1-6, Bronze 1-2, Super Bronze, Red Bull) appear on older maps; the new names are Hungaroring, Grid 1-3, T1, Pit Exit 1-2, Grand Prix 1-2, Apex 1-2, Chicane 1-4, Fan, 3 Corner.
- **Variants:** no other layouts are used. A karting centre and the Driving Center are separate facilities on site.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** the pit lane is on the **right-hand side** of the main straight in the direction of travel (FIA 2025 and 2026 notes):
  - passing to the right of the SC1 bollard counts as pit entry;
  - pit-exit cars stay to the right of the continuous white line;
  - practice starts are on the right of the pit exit road;
  - grid panels are on the right.

  The main (Hungaroring) grandstand is on the left, opposite the garages.
- **Pit entry:** on the right at the final corner (T14), visible from the Apex and Grand Prix stands. A yellow flag on the right of the entry road covers incidents there.
- **Pit exit:** on the right, towards T1. A continuous white line runs on past SC2, so pit-exit cars rejoin along the right of the approach to T1, and the dotted line across the exit road marks the track edge. The Pit Exit 1-2 grandstands are on the outside (left), between the end of the pit lane and T1.
- **Start / finish:** the race finish is the Control Line on the track. The FIA map marks a separate START LINE and CONTROL LINE; their offset is not stated. The grid has 28 boxes, with grid-removal gates next to positions 6 and 18 (pit wall side).
- **Pit building (2025):** on the right of the straight, on a ridge.
  - 36 garages and 4 technical garages.
  - A VIP area of 2,000 over the pit lane: the Paddock Club, which since 2026 has more than 3,800 seats above the garages.
  - Rooftop terrace and press conference room.
  - At least three floors. Length and storeys are not published.
  - Race control and the media centre are permanent in the main building since 2026. Their exact position in the building is unconfirmed.
- **Paddock:** behind the pit building (right side), 8,000 m² since 2024, on the ridge above the valley. The F2/F3 paddock is a separate area marked on the wayfinding map; position unconfirmed.
- **Tunnels:** two tunnels under the start/finish straight link the paddock and the main grandstand (2024).
- **Podium:** "Podium Club" hospitality is near the Pit Exit stands on the wayfinding map. The podium position is unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
- The circuit lies in a natural amphitheatre / valley ("Valley of the Three Springs"), and about 80% of the track is visible from any point.
- **Elevation change:** 34.7 m (F1.com) or about 36 m (F1 Chronicle).
- **Highest:** the start/finish straight, where the paddock sits on the ridge.
- **Downhill:** the main straight falls towards T1, and the track keeps descending through T2-T3. T4 climbs over a crest.
- **Lowest:** around T5-T6 (F1 Chronicle). The track then climbs back through T6-T9.
- **Final corner:** T14 is approached downhill.

## Key coordinates
- **Circuit centre:** 47.5822 N, 19.2511 E (Wikipedia: 47°34′56″N 19°15′04″E). OSM: https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=16/47.5822/19.2511 (link constructed; OSM pages could not be opened).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: **unconfirmed.** The pit entry is at T14 on the right, the pit exit is on the right before T1, and the main straight runs about 908 m from T14 to T1.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **Use the 2003 layout** (908 m straight, tight T1, tightened T12). Pre-2003 maps show a 3.975 km track with a shorter straight.
- **The 1986-88 chicane after T3 is gone.**
- **The pit building and main grandstand are new (2025-2026).** Pre-2024 photos show the old 1980s pit building and the small paddock.
  - The new pit building has 36 garages and Paddock Club floors above the pit lane with a rooftop terrace.
  - The main grandstand is covered.
  - Two tunnels run under the straight.
- **Pit lane is on the right.** The pit exit runs alongside the straight and merges before T1, behind a continuous white line.
- **Corner names are new (2026).** Older material uses only numbers, or informal names for T1-T4, T11-T13.
- **Two naming schemes for grandstands:** old (Gold/Silver/Bronze) and new (Grid/Pit Exit/Chicane/Apex/Grand Prix). Use the new scheme.
- **Run-off:** T5 exit is gravel (since 2025). Concrete walls run on the left at T14.
