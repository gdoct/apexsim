# Shanghai International Circuit (`Shanghaied`)

Modelled on: Jiading District, north-west Shanghai, China (purpose-built on former swamp/rice paddies, Tilke, opened 2004; layout shaped like the character 上 "shang"). In game: 5.43 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.451 km, 16 turns (7 left, 9 right), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Grand Prix Circuit, 2004-present: 5.451 km, 16 turns.** The F1 layout has never changed. The only geometric change on site (2012) smoothed the double hairpins at the end of the West circuit, which the GP lap does not use. The track was fully resurfaced in August 2024 (bumps had earlier been repaired with a 3D-printing method). F1 returned in 2024 after a five-year gap.
- **In-game 5.43 km matches the GP layout** (21 m short).
- Other layouts: Motorcycle GP 5.281 km (less severe T5-T6 complex), Intermediate 4.603 km (bypasses the T5-T6 section; WTCC/V8 Supercars), West Long 3.051 km (Formula E uses a long West version), West Short 2.201 km, three East Handling circuits.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane on the driver's right of the main straight**, Grandstand A (main, ~29,000-30,000 seats) along almost the entire straight on the left, opposite the pits. Pole position on the left: the 2024 FIA pit lane drawing labels "Pole LHS" (f1-fansite says right; trust the FIA drawing).
- **Pit entry**: on the right before T16; drivers stay right of the solid white line; may cross the dotted line on the left but not overtake there; crossing right of the solid line after SC1 = entering the pits. Marshal post 16.2 on the right of the pit entry road.
- **Pit exit**: a separate pit exit road; the painted pit exit line continues past the SC2 line, so cars run alongside the track before merging (merge point unconfirmed - believed after T1-T2). Practice starts on the asphalt to the right of the pit exit before the end of the pit wall. Pit loss ~21 s.
- **Pit building / team buildings / paddock**: behind the pit garages, each F1 team has its own building; the team offices stand on stilts over a lake, arranged like pavilions in Shanghai's Yu Garden. This lake-and-pavilion area is the paddock.
- **Race Control tower** (the control tower, at the main complex): Media Centre on its 9th floor (200 seats, photographers' area inside), entered from the paddock. **Podium Building** next to the control tower; press conference room on its 2nd floor.
- **Wing bridges**: wing-like viewing platforms of the main grandstand complex cross the circuit at either end of the main straight (bridges over the start/finish straight). The circuit lists a "Sky Restaurant and a media centre above the track" - which wing holds which is unconfirmed.
- **Start line vs finish line**: not documented as separate.

## Terrain and elevation
Flat reclaimed swamp; total elevation change about 7 m (f1-fansite). Gradients up to 3 % uphill and 8 % downhill (FIA media kit). T2-T4 run downhill; T7 runs uphill. T12-T13 slightly banked.

## Key coordinates
- Circuit (Wikipedia): 31.33889, 121.21972 - https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=16/31.3389/121.2197
- Start line, pit entry, pit exit: unconfirmed (OSM not queryable). Relative: pit entry on the right before T16; pit exit road on the right towards T1-T2.

## Modelling pitfalls
- The "snails": T1-T2 is one long closing-radius right that turns back ~270 degrees; T11-T13 is the opposite (opening radius) onto the back straight. Both are easy to model as simple corners.
- Wing-shaped grandstand bridges span the main straight at both ends; the main grandstand roof is a distinctive lotus/wing shape.
- The paddock is a lake with team pavilions on stilts, not a tarmac paddock.
- The 1.175-1.2 km back straight ends in a wide hairpin (T14) with H and K grandstands facing each other.
- Several layouts share the site; the GP lap uses the full T5-T6 complex (not the bike or Intermediate version).
- Separate pit exit road that runs alongside the track before merging.
