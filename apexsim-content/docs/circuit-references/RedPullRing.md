# Red Bull Ring (`RedPullRing`)

Modelled on: Red Bull Ring, Spielberg, Murtal, Styria, Austria (operator Projekt Spielberg). About 677 m above sea level. In game: 4.31 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.326 km declared since 2025 (4.318 km in 2016-2024, same tarmac), 10 turns (7 right, 3 left), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current GP layout:** the A1-Ring layout (Hermann Tilke, 1996) as rebuilt in 2011 as the Red Bull Ring.
  - Declared 4.318 km for 2016-2024, then **4.326 km from 2025** after an FIA laser rescan.
  - The Race (2025) explains the +8 m: there was no circuit modification. It is either ground movement or the centreline moving outward where corner exits were widened, e.g. the final-corner exit kerb moved out.
  - **The in-game 4.31 km matches this layout; it is not a different configuration.**
- History (Wikipedia):
  - Zeltweg airfield circuit (1964).
  - Österreichring 1969-1987: 5.911 km, later 5.941 km with the Hella-Licht chicane.
  - 1988-1995: 5.852 km after the Bosch Kurve modification.
  - A1-Ring 1996-2004: 4.326 km, 9 turns. The fast sweepers of the Österreichring were replaced by tight right-handers on the same hillside.
  - 2004: Red Bull bought the circuit and demolished the pits and grandstands.
  - 2011: rebuilt for about EUR 70 m and reopened on 15 May 2011. F1 returned in 2014.
- **Other variants:**
  - South loop (Südschleife), 2.336 km.
  - Motorcycle layout (2022+), 4.348 km, with a bikes-only chicane at T2 (the "Münzer Turn / Chicane").
  - The old North (Nord) club loop is no longer used.
- **2025 changes:** T2 to T3 exit resurfaced. White lines realigned, with blue lines behind them, at the exits of T1, T3, T4, T6 and T8 and the entry of T7.
- **2026 changes** (FIA notes):
  - High inner kerbs at the T3 and T10 apexes removed.
  - New fences at T1-T2 to protect the advertising bridge.
  - The opening on the right of T1 closed.
  - Extra tyre rows at T2 and T4.
  - Smoother off-track transition at T9-T10 right.
  - **Pit wall extended at the start of the straight.**
  - Pit-entry speed-limit line about 18 m earlier; pit-exit line moved about 23 m.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** the pit lane is on the **right** of the main straight in the direction of travel.
  - Pit-entry cars keep right of the white line from the 50 m board.
  - Grid panels are on the right.
  - The Start-Ziel grandstand is on the left, facing the garages (Oversteer48).
- **Pit entry:** on the right after T10, the final corner. Gate 6 serves the pit-entry area.
- **Pit exit:** on the right towards T1, separated by a white line. Cars must not cross it except on reconnaissance laps. The exact merge point (before or after T1) is **unconfirmed**. The Steiermark stand at T1 has "the best view of pit exit".
- **Start vs finish:** the **start line is offset 126-127 m** from the control/finish line (The Race; GT2 European Series briefing). Seen from Start-Ziel:
  - the finish line is opposite sectors E-F;
  - the start line is opposite sector K;
  - the podium is opposite sectors H-I, on the pit-building side.

  The grid has 34 boxes, with car-removal gates next to the start line and grid position 12.
- **Pit building:** right side, garages from opposite Start-Ziel sector H to sector M.
  - The **F1 Paddock Club** is above the garages.
  - A giant screen sits on top of the pit building.
  - Storeys and length are unconfirmed.
- **Media centre:** in the **voestalpine Wing**, a steel structure shaped like an F1 rear wing, 92 m x 20 m, about 900 t of steel, holding about 500 people. It sits above and behind the Start-Ziel grandstand on the left of the straight, overlooking the start/finish (Black Book Motorsport; EnterF1; Lectura).
- **Paddock:** behind the pit building (right side), with the F1 Paddock and a support paddock ("Paddock" plus "F1 Paddock" on the wayfinding map). Exact extent unconfirmed.
- **Race control:** position unconfirmed (not on the maps fetched).

## Terrain and elevation
- **Elevation change:** about 63-65 m (FIA 2026 media kit: 65 m; F1.com 2016: 63.5 m; F1.com 2026: 63 m).
- **Altitude:** 677 m above sea level.
- **Gradients:** max uphill 12%, max downhill 9.3%.
- **Lowest:** around the start/finish straight.
- **The climb:** the straight climbs to T1, then the track keeps climbing past T2 to the highest point around T2-T3.
- **The descent:** a long downhill run from T3 to T4, then further down through T5-T6. T9 is downhill into the final corners.
- The whole circuit sits on a hillside facing the Mur valley.

## Key coordinates
- **Circuit centre:** 47.2197 N, 14.7647 E (Wikipedia: 47°13′11″N 14°45′53″E). OSM: https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=16/47.2197/14.7647 (link constructed; OSM pages could not be opened).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: **unconfirmed.** The pit entry is after T10 on the right, the pit exit is towards T1 on the right, and the start line is about 126 m from the control line.
- OSM raceway relation or way id: not found.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **The 4.318 / 4.326 km difference is a remeasurement**, not a layout change. Do not "fix" the in-game 4.31 km by altering corners.
- **Do not use Österreichring corners.** Hella-Licht, Bosch Kurve and the fast sweepers are pre-1996; the A1-Ring (1996-2003) layout is the same as today.
- **The bikes-only T2 chicane (2022+) is not part of the F1 layout.**
- **Start and finish lines differ** by about 126 m. The podium is on the pit side.
- **Pit lane on the right**, with Start-Ziel and the voestalpine Wing (media centre) on the left. The pit wall was extended at the start of the straight in 2026.
- **The advertising bridge between T1 and T2 is a real structure over the track.**
- **Steep gradients** (12% up to T1, 9.3% down): a flat model will be badly wrong. T3 is the high point.
