# Suzuka International Racing Course (`Sudoku`)

Modelled on: Suzuka Circuit, Ino-cho, Suzuka City, Mie Prefecture, Japan (owner Honda, operator Honda Mobilityland). In game: 5.80 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.807 km, 18 turns (10 right, 8 left), figure-of-eight: the first half (T1-T10) runs clockwise, the second half (T11-T18) anticlockwise, joined by a crossover bridge.

## Which layout
- **Current F1 configuration: 5.807 km, unchanged since 2003** (Honda 2026 preview). The in-game 5.80 km matches it.
- History (Wikipedia):
  - 1962: built as a Honda test track to a John Hugenholtz design. The original layout was 6.004 km with 18 bends and a crossover (FIA 2024 media kit).
  - 1983: a chicane (the "Casio Triangle") was added at the last curve.
  - 1984: the first part of Spoon was made slower.
  - 1985: the first corner was made slower.
  - 1987: upgraded for F1, with Degner split into two corners.
  - 2002: 130R was rebuilt as a double radius (85 m then 340 m), the S-curves were straightened slightly, and Dunlop run-off went from 12 m to 25 m.
  - 2003: the chicane was made slightly faster and moved closer to 130R. This produced the current 5.807 km.
- **Motorcycle layout** (5.821 km): has a different final chicane and an extra chicane between the Hairpin and 200R, both added in 2003.
- **Recent surface and run-off changes:**
  - 2014: Dunlop was slightly modified.
  - 2025: resurfaced from T1 to T8 entry. Gravel replaced artificial grass outside T2, T7, T9, T14 and T17, and T9 got a double kerb.
  - 2026: resurfaced from T7 to T17, with new debris fencing at T13.
- **Variants:**
  - East Course: the pit straight, then T1 to the first half of Dunlop, then a tight right-hander back to the pit straight (about 2.24 km).
  - West Course: the rest of the lap including the crossover. Its grid is on the straight leading to the overpass.
- **Corner sponsor names now used by the circuit:** NIPPO Corner (T7, historically Dunlop), NISSIN Brake Hairpin (T11), Astemo Chicane (T16-17, formerly Casio Triangle, then Hitachi Automotive Systems Chicane). Older maps use the older names.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** the pit lane runs on the **right-hand side** of the main straight in the direction of travel. The main grandstand (V1/V2) is on the left.
  - FIA 2019 event notes: drivers keep to the right of the pit-entry bollard and the right of the pit-exit line, practice starts are on the right-hand side, and car number panels are on the right of the grid.
- **Pit entry:** on the right after the Final Curve (T18), at the start of the main straight. It is visible from Q2 block 1 and from the R/S stands.
- **Pit exit:** on the right. A solid white line keeps leaving cars to its right, and a dotted line across the exit marks the track edge. Cars rejoin on the right of the main straight before T1. The exact merge point is unconfirmed; the notes mention the pit signalling wall end and "the grass verge after the old medical centre".
- **Start vs finish:** the **start line is about 300 m nearer T1 than the control (finish) line** (Honda; F1technical "start/finish offset 0.300 km"). The finish line is on the sloping part of the straight.
- **Garage order:** garages run "in reverse": the top teams are at the far (T1 / pit-exit) end (thef1spectator).
- **Pit building:** rebuilt 2009 by Takenaka for F1's return.
  - Steel, 3 storeys, 40,687 m² including the grandstand. The grandstand is 350 m long.
  - It sits on the straight's 2.8% slope, which is absorbed inside the building.
  - 1F: garages, Medical Centre, Scrutineering.
  - 2F: Media Centre, Briefing Room, Race Control, Stewards and Timing (FIA 2024 media kit), plus a Hospitality Lounge with units 01-11.
  - 3F: Hospitality Terrace with units 01-12 (official grandstand PDF).
  - The F1 Paddock Club is listed at the "Home Straight (T0)" and occupies these upper floors above the pit lane.
- **Control tower, podium, Podium Media Center, Flag Tower, Signal:** all labelled on the official PDF and the FIA map around the pit building and start line. The podium faces the main grandstand. Exact positions are unconfirmed.
- **Paddock:** behind the pit building, on the right of the straight.
  - Team garages are numbered 1-55 on the FIA map.
  - The map also shows a separate Porsche (support) paddock, DO & CO, the TV Compound and a "Rotary".
  - The **West Paddock** serves as national media parking and the **Honda Racing School (HRS) office** is near the West Gate. Their exact positions relative to the track are unconfirmed.
- **Paddock Tunnel and lifts:** labelled on the official grandstand PDF. A pedestrian tunnel links the grandstand side and the paddock under the main straight; its exact position along the straight is unconfirmed.

## Terrain and elevation
- **Total elevation change: 40.4 m** (F1.com).
- **Highest point:** just after Spoon (T13/T14).
- **Lowest point:** between T1 and T2.
- **Main straight:** falls about 2.8% towards T1 (Takenaka).
- After T2 the track climbs through the Esses: T5 is over a crest, T6 is at the bottom of the S section, and Dunlop (T7) climbs uphill and blind.
- Degner 1 has a dip.
- The Hairpin is banked.
- The track drops away on the entry to Spoon.
- The back straight runs over the bridge and down towards 130R and the chicane.

## Key coordinates
- **Circuit centre:** about 34.8417 N, 136.5389 E (Wikipedia infobox). OSM: https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=16/34.8417/136.5389 (link constructed from these coordinates; OSM pages could not be opened by the research tools).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: **unconfirmed.** Take them from OSM or aerial imagery around the main straight. The pit entry is right after T18, the pit exit is near the T1 end on the right, and the start line is about 300 m ahead of the finish line.

## Modelling pitfalls
- **Separate start and finish lines:** the start line is about 300 m ahead of the control/finish line.
- **Pit lane is on the right.** Keep the grandstand on the left of the straight and the pit building on the right.
- **The crossover must be a real bridge.** The West Straight goes over the T9-T10 section, and the lower road runs under it.
- **Chicane shape:**
  - The car chicane (T16-17) is right-left and since 2003 sits closer to 130R than on 1987-2002 maps.
  - Do not use the motorcycle chicane, which has a different final chicane plus an extra chicane after the Hairpin.
  - 130R is the post-2002 double radius, not the old single 130 m arc.
- **Corner names changed:** NIPPO Corner = Dunlop, NISSIN Brake Hairpin, Astemo Chicane = Casio Triangle / Hitachi chicane.
- **Run-off:** gravel (not artificial grass) outside T2, T7, T9, T14 and T17 since 2025.
- **Main straight slope:** it falls towards T1, and the pit building absorbs the slope.
- **The Ferris wheel matters.** It is the defining off-track landmark, behind the main grandstand.
