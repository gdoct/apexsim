# Circuit Gilles Villeneuve, Montreal, Canada (`ChillyVilleneuve`)

Modelled on: Île Notre-Dame, Parc Jean-Drapeau, Montreal, Quebec. In game: 4.35 km, F1. Real (current layout): 4.361 km, 14 turns, clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Current layout (2002-present), 4.361 km, 14 turns.** The 2002 pit-exit change produced today's length.
  - Kerbs at the final chicane were changed in 2005.
  - The pit lane was lengthened in 2007-08 to fit NASCAR's 43-car field.
  - In 2017 the exit of the final chicane (Wall of Champions) was re-angled and Tecpro barriers were added.
- **Paddock and pit building (the "2026 new pit building?" question).** The new building is not from 2026. The old 1988 paddock was demolished after the 2018 race. A new three-storey paddock building by Les Architectes FABG was inaugurated on 15 May 2019 and has been used since the 2019 GP.
- **2026:** no layout change. The 2026 Race Director notes list only:
  - a smoother T1 apex kerb, and drainage cuts plus a blue line at T2;
  - a wall realigned on the right at T6, with a new opening on the left at T6;
  - walls realigned at the T13 run-off and at the T14 rejoin, with the white and blue lines at the T14 exit extended;
  - gates fitted in the pit-wall debris fence;
  - a dashed line at pit entry.
  The 2026 FIA circuit map (v2/v3, 22.05.26) gives a 4.361 km centreline.
- **2024:** full resurface. The track was lowered between T7 and T8 to give at least 4.0 m clearance under the Concorde Bridge, and the pit exit opening before T2 was moved downstream.
- **Earlier configurations:**
  - 1978-86: 4.410 km, 19 turns. Pits and start/finish were at the hairpin.
  - 1986/88-93: 4.430 km, 17 turns. Pits and start/finish moved to the exit of the final chicane (the start line moved west).
  - 1994-95: 4.450 km, 20 turns, with a chicane added between Casino corner and the hairpin.
  - 1996-2001: 4.421 km, 13 turns. The 1994 chicane and the Casino corner were removed, making the run from the hairpin the Casino Straight.
- **The in-game 4.35 km matches the 2002-present layout** (4.361 km).

## Corners
Directions are cross-checked from several sources:
- Alex Wurz's lap (F1technical) and the TrackTitan positioning advice.
- FIA track-limit notes: T9 and T13 are cut by going left of the apex kerb.
- McLaren: "T13 right-hander ... right-left chicane".

Some secondary sites (Sportskeeda, f1da) contradict each other and are wrong on several corners. Sportskeeda wrongly calls the track anticlockwise.

There is one unresolved conflict. McLaren and Oversteer48 give the final chicane as right-left (T13 R, T14 L, wall on the right). The FIA T13 track-limit wording ("left of the orange apex kerb") and "T13 run-off on the right" suggest the opposite. Verify T13/T14 directions on the F1 or FIA map before modelling.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit lane side:** On the left (outside, Olympic Basin side) of the start/finish straight. Grandstand 1 is on the inside (right) of the main straight, directly opposite the pit lane and pit building. Grandstand 17 (Alpine) is beside it, and the Platine covered grandstand is between the grid and T1.
- **Pit entry:** Off the Casino Straight before the final chicane (T13). FIA drawings show "Pit Entry" with a dashed line before Safety Car Line 1 (2026). The pit entry road runs inside the chicane; its side is most likely the left (unconfirmed). Drivers keep left of the solid white line on the right-hand side.
- **Pit exit:** A long pit exit road on the left alongside T1, passing round the outside of T2, rejoining after T2 on the left. The "opening before Turn 2 on the left" was moved downstream in 2024. Recon-lap practice starts are from painted boxes on the left after pit exit.
- **Garages:** 2024 order (from one end): Haas, Sauber, RB, Williams, Alpine, Aston Martin, McLaren, Ferrari, Mercedes, Red Bull. Boxes 1-43, FOM, F1 VIP walkway. The FIA notes call the pit lane narrow.
- **Start/finish:** The start line and podium are in front of the right-hand end of Grandstand 1. "Salut Gilles" is painted on the track at the start line, and a photographers' tower faces the podium.
- **Paddock building (2019):** Les Architectes FABG, three storeys, about 22,235 m², about C$47-50 M.
  - Contents: open-plan modular garages, FIA offices, a race control tower spread horizontally over two floors, an integrated media centre / press room, and corporate loges for 5,000 on two storeys.
  - Covered panoramic terrace on the top floor, and an exposed CLT wood roof structure (nod to Expo 67) with solar panels.
  - Two front entrances and a rear service entrance to the hospitality zone; freight elevator at the south end.
  - Three TV screens are mounted on top of the building facing Grandstand 1.
  - Length is not published.
- **Support paddock:** Along the Olympic Basin, served by a basin shuttle; reachable from the Grandstand 24 area.
- **Media centre:** In the paddock complex, near the media drop-off and parking P-41.

## Terrain and elevation
Low and largely flat on the man-made island, with only small undulations: a slight drop into the T1 braking zone and downhill braking into the hairpin. No published elevation profile was found; the range is a few metres (unconfirmed).

## Key coordinates
- Circuit reference: 45.5006 N, 73.5225 W (Wikipedia, 45°30′02″N 73°31′21″W).
- OSM area: https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=15/45.5006/-73.5260 (no raceway way or relation id found).
- Start line, pit entry and pit exit: not individually confirmed. The start line is at the west end of the pit building, per the "Salut Gilles" marking in front of the right-hand end of GS1.

## Modelling pitfalls
- The pits are on the outside (left). The pit exit is a long separate road around T1/T2 rejoining after T2 (since 2002); older maps show the pre-2002 exit.
- The final chicane direction and the Wall of Champions side: see the conflict note above Corners. Verify against the FIA map.
- The paddock building is the 2019 building (a long three-storey structure with a wood roof). Pre-2019 imagery shows the old 1988 garages.
- No chicane between the hairpin and T13 since 1996. Pre-1996 maps show the Casino corner and the 1994 chicane.
- The Concorde Bridge crosses the track before T8. Clearance is tight (4 m after lowering in 2024).
- Track width is narrow, with walls close everywhere (a semi-street circuit on park roads). Kerbs are tall sausage and "apex" kerbs at the chicanes.
- 2026 F1 uses Straight Mode zones (A1 main straight, A2 after T7, A3 after T9, A4 after T11). Older maps show DRS zones.
