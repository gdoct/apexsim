# Bahrain International Circuit, Sakhir (`Bahrainless`)

Modelled on: Sakhir, Southern Governorate, Bahrain (purpose-built desert circuit, Tilke, opened 2004). In game: 5.39 km, F1. Real (current layout): 5.412 km (Grand Prix Circuit), 15 turns (6 left, 9 right), clockwise.

## Which layout
- **Grand Prix Circuit (2005-present): 5.412 km, 15 turns.** Used for F1 2004-2009 and every year since 2012 (2010 used the Endurance layout). The 2004 original was 5.417 km; Turn 4 was realigned before 2005, shortening the lap by 5 m. Since 2014 the Grand Prix is a night race under permanent floodlights. No layout change since 2005; kerb/run-off detail changes (e.g. Turn 3 right-hand kerb removed and Turn 4 exit gravel extended for 2025 support events, per FIA F3 event notes).
- **The in-game 5.39 km matches the GP layout** (22 m short; within modelling tolerance) - not the Endurance or Outer layouts.
- **Other layouts on the same site** (all share parts of the GP track):
  - Endurance Circuit 6.299 km, 23 turns (F1 2010; WEC uses the GP layout).
  - Outer Circuit 3.543 km, 11 turns (2020 Sakhir GP): leaves the GP track after T4 and rejoins at the back straight before T13/T14, skipping the infield.
  - Paddock Circuit 3.823 km, 10 turns; Oasis/Inner Circuit 2.550 km, 8 turns.
  - 1.2 km concrete drag strip; 2.5 km flat test oval with two 180-degree banked bends. Positions of drag strip/oval relative to the GP track: unconfirmed.
- The 2026 Bahrain GP was not held at Sakhir (Middle East conflict): F1 ran it at Sepang, Malaysia, on 2-4 October 2026 (see `Shebang.md`). The most recent F1 race at Sakhir is 2025.

## Start/finish, pit lane, paddock
- **Pit straight ~1.09-1.1 km**, the start/finish line on it. Pole position on the right of the grid (f1-fansite).
- **Pit lane on the driver's right** (inside), opposite the Main Grandstand which is on the left.
- **Pit entry**: on the right in the T14-T15 complex; drivers must keep right of the bollard; red-and-white hatched area between pit entry road and track may not be crossed once committed (FIA notes).
- **Pit exit**: on the right; drivers must keep to the right of the solid white line at pit exit (no crossing); the dotted line across the pit exit is the track edge. Rejoins on the inside towards T1 (exact merge point unconfirmed). Blue-flag marshal at pit exit. Pit speed limit 60 km/h (support events 2025; F1 value unconfirmed). Pit loss ~22.5 s.
- **Pit building**: permanent, partly two and partly three storeys, 18,000 m2, tent-like PTFE fabric roofs. FIA pit lane drawing labels "PIT BUILDING" on both sides of the pit lane (garages one side, team storage the other). Three TV screens on the pit building facing the main grandstand.
- **Podium**: at the start (entry end) of the pit garages, opposite the main grandstand (oversteer48).
- **Paddock**: 58,362 m2 "oasis" area behind the pit building (inside the circuit, at the start of the lap); six one-storey team buildings (5,000 m2), garages to F1 standard, medical centre, broadcast centre, media centre (500 journalists).
- **Second pit building / support race pit lane**: a separate support pit lane exists (used as emergency route to the Medical Centre); a second pit building (multipurpose, three storeys, 12,000 m2, accommodates 6,000 spectators, lounges and a 3,000-seat grandstand) - this is understood to be the building carrying the Beyon (Batelco) grandstand behind the main pit building facing T7-T10 (position partly unconfirmed).
- **Race control / media centre**: exact building unconfirmed (likely in the pit building or the main grandstand complex).

## Terrain and elevation
Relief 0-18 m (crash.net); f1-fansite gives 17 m change; starting grid averages 7.45 m, highest point 25 m (Gulf Construction). Maximum climb 3.6 %, maximum descent 5.6 %. Climb from T3 up to T4 (crest at T4); drop through T8 hairpin and downhill braking into T10; climb through T12-T13.

## Key coordinates
- Circuit (Wikipedia): 26.03250, 50.51056 - https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=16/26.0325/50.5106
- Start line, pit entry, pit exit: unconfirmed (OSM could not be queried). Relative: start line on the main straight opposite the Main Grandstand; pit entry at T14/T15 on the right; pit exit on the right near T1.

## Modelling pitfalls
- Several layouts share tarmac: the GP track must take the infield (T5-T10) and **not** the Outer Circuit link after T4, nor the Endurance loop.
- 2004 Turn 4 differs from the current one (realigned 2005).
- The site has two pit lanes (F1 and support); the F1 pit is on the right of the main straight.
- Turn 1 is a near-hairpin right, Turn 8 a right-hand hairpin, Turns 9-10 a double left with downhill braking.
- Buildings use white tent-like fabric roofs (desert theme); the Sakhir Tower is the key landmark.
- Night race: floodlight masts all round the track.
